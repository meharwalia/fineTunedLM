You can sharpen a pencil without a pencil sharpener in these ways:

1. **Knife or blade:** Use a sharp knife or blade. Carefully shave away the wood to expose the graphite core. Rotate the pencil while you shave to get an even point.
2. **Sandpaper:** Rub the tip of the pencil against fine-grit sandpaper at a slight angle. Continue until the tip is sharp. This method gives precise control over the sharpness of the point.
3. **Nail file:** Similar to sandpaper, gently file the tip of the pencil with a nail file. Continue until the tip reaches the sharpness you want.
4. **Razor blade:** If you have one, carefully shave the wood away from the pencil tip. Do not cut yourself.

These methods need patience and precision. They can sharpen a pencil without a traditional sharpener.
