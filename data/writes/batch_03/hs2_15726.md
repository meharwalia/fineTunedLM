One day, a man named Wilson worked at the bowling alley. He checked people in. Two very large men walked up to the counter.

They wore tattoos and leather jackets. They were frightening, with more metal piercings in their faces than a board of nails. Their mouths were turned into snarls.

Wilson was extremely intimidated. He checked them in as quickly as possible, so as not to make them mad. After they left to bowl, Wilson sighed with relief and continued his job.

On his way home that night, a man with a knife confronted Wilson on the dark sidewalk. The man threatened Wilson. He asked for all of his belongings, or else Wilson would get hurt.

Wilson began to fear for his life. Then two large men stepped out of the shadows and wrestled the attacker to the ground. After the attacker stopped moving, Wilson got a good look at his saviors.

They were the two large men from the bowling alley!

The cops came and arrested the thief. Wilson thanked the two large men again and again. He even invited them to dinner.

Wilson and the two men walked away. It was the start of a fruitful friendship.
