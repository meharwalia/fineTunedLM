Both Florida and Tennessee offer unique and exciting experiences for couples on a Valentine's Day vacation.

**Florida**

In the subtropics of Florida, you can enjoy warm weather, beautiful beaches and exciting theme parks. Westgate Lakes Resort & Spa in Orlando offers luxurious accommodations. Its amenities include a spa and a sports bar.

**Tennessee**

In the mountains of Tennessee, you can enjoy breathtaking views, outdoor activities and a more relaxed atmosphere. Westgate Smoky Mountain Resort & Water Park in Gatlinburg offers spacious villa accommodations. Its activities include a water park and mini golf.

The choice depends on your personal preferences and what you look for in a Valentine's Day vacation. Both destinations offer a unique and memorable experience. You can choose either one.
