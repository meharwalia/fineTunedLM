The structure of a Google AdWords account, from top to bottom:

1. **Account level:** The top level. It contains the settings and information for your entire account.
2. **Campaign level:** Each campaign in your account is a separate level. It has its own settings, budget and targeting options.
3. **Ad group level:** In each campaign, you can create multiple ad groups. They organize your ads and keywords by theme or product.
4. **Keyword level:** Each ad group has a list of keywords. They trigger your ads to show.
5. **Ad level:** Each ad group has one or more ads. Users see them when they search for keywords related to your ad group.
6. **Landing page level:** Each ad links to a specific landing page on your website. Users go to this page when they click your ad.

Summary: Account Level > Campaign Level > Ad Group Level > Keyword Level > Ad Level > Landing Page Level.
