The fourth dimension is a concept in mathematics and physics. It is an extra dimension of space or time beyond the three dimensions of width, height and depth.

In the context of space, people often call the fourth dimension the time dimension. It represents the passage of time.

It describes how objects and events change over time. You can picture it as a "time-like" axis that runs parallel to the three spatial dimensions.

Works of fiction explore the idea. One example is the novel "A Wrinkle in Time" by Madeleine L'Engle. It has a character who can travel through the fourth dimension.

The concept is purely theoretical. No evidence shows that you can experience it in the same way as the three spatial dimensions.
