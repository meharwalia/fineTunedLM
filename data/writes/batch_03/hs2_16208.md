These are the most prestigious music awards, with their origins, standards, impact and controversies.

**Grammy Award**

- Established in 1959. People widely regard it as the pinnacle of musical achievement, like the Oscars in the film industry.
- It covers a diverse range of genres, from classical to contemporary sounds. Recording Academy members select winners in a meritocratic peer vote.
- Allegations of bias and lack of transparency have put the selection process under scrutiny.
- Winning is highly coveted. It can mark a turning point in a career. It significantly enhances the credibility and visibility of the artist.

**American Music Award (AMA)**

- Dick Clark founded it in 1973. He created it to fill the void when the Grammy Awards were not on television that year.
- Unlike the Grammys, public vote decides the AMAs. This reflects the preferences of the music-listening public.
- People praise this populist approach for its inclusivity. Critics say it can overlook artistic merit in favor of commercial popularity.

**BRIT Awards**

- Originated in 1977. They celebrate the achievements of British and international artists.
- The Voting Academy selects winners. It has over 1,000 members from sectors of the music industry.
- The BRITs played a crucial role in elevating artists to global fame. They have faced controversy over representation and diversity among nominees and winners.

**Billboard Music Awards**

- First presented in 1990. They are unique because they use actual performance on the Billboard charts. The data include digital sales, streaming, radio airplay and social engagement.
- This quantitative approach gives a clear measure of commercial success. Critics say it can overlook the artistic quality and innovation of music that does not reach mainstream popularity.

**MTV Video Music Award (VMA)**

- Launched in 1984. It honors the best in music videos, a medium that MTV itself helped popularize.
- Professional juries and public votes together select winners.
- The VMAs are known for their celebration of creativity and innovation in music video production. Critics say they favor certain genres and overlook others. Controversial moments have sometimes overshadowed the achievements the VMAs honor.

**Ivor Novello Awards**

- They have recognized excellence in British and Irish songwriting and composing since 1955. They focus on the artistry behind music creation.
- The songwriting and composing community selects winners. This keeps the awards focused on musical creativity and talent.
- The awards do not cover the broader aspects of music production and performance. This gives a narrower scope of recognition.

**Echo Awards**

- They were Germany's premier music award from 1992 until their discontinuation in 2018. They recognized national and international achievements based on sales figures.
- The organizers disbanded the awards after significant backlash. The backlash followed a prize for a duo with lyrics that people deemed offensive.
- This shows the challenge that award organizations face to balance commercial success with ethical standards.
