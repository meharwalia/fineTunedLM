**Matches and sets**

A tennis match is typically the best of three sets. The first player to win two sets wins the match.

The first player to win at least six games, with a two-game advantage over the opponent, wins a set. If the score reaches six games all, a tiebreak game decides the set.

**Games and points**

In a game, one player serves and the opponent returns the serve. A player wins a point when the opponent cannot return the ball. A player also wins a point when the opponent makes a mistake, such as hitting the ball into the net or out of bounds.

The announcer says the score of the server first. The score of the opponent follows.

The possible scores in a game:

- 0: Love
- 15: 15
- 2: 30
- 3: 40
- 4: Game (if the player wins the point)

**Deuce**

If the score is tied at 40-40, it is "deuce." The player who wins the next point has the advantage. That player needs one more point to win the game.

If that player loses the point, the score goes back to deuce. From deuce, a player must win two consecutive points to win the game.

**Tiebreak**

In a tiebreak game, the player who served first in the set serves the first point. The players change sides after every six points.

The first player to reach seven points, with at least a two-point lead, wins the tiebreak and the set. If the score reaches six points all, a player must win two consecutive points to win the tiebreak and the set.
