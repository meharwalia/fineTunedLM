**Slide 1: Title**

"Adapting Amazon's Indian Strategy to Solve Ethiopia's E-commerce Logistics Problem"

**Slide 2: Problem**

- Ethiopia lacks reliable logistics and delivery infrastructure. This makes e-commerce difficult to scale.
- Low financial inclusion and internet penetration limit the potential customer base.

**Slide 3: Solution**

- Partner with local logistics and delivery companies to create a reliable delivery network.
- Add cash-on-delivery options for the large unbanked population.
- Offer local language support and a user-friendly mobile interface to increase accessibility.

**Slide 4: Business Model**

- Marketplace model with third-party sellers.
- Commission-based revenue model.
- Additional services for sellers, such as warehousing and fulfillment.

**Slide 5: Market**

- Estimated population of 110 million, with a large youth population and a growing middle class.
- The e-commerce market is small now, but it has potential for growth.
- High mobile phone penetration and increasing internet usage.

**Slide 6: Competitors, Competitive Advantage**

- Current competitors are local e-commerce platforms and informal markets.
- Competitive advantages:
  - Reliable logistics and delivery network.
  - User-friendly interface and local language support.
  - Cash-on-delivery options and financial inclusion initiatives.

**Slide 7: Business Plan**

- Year 1:
  - Build partnerships with local logistics and delivery companies.
  - Launch the platform with a select group of sellers.
  - Invest in marketing and user acquisition.
- Year 2:
  - Expand the seller base and product range.
  - Improve logistics and delivery efficiency.
  - Increase brand awareness and customer acquisition.
- Year 3:
  - Explore expansion to other cities and regions.
  - Invest in innovative solutions to improve logistics and delivery further.
  - Evaluate potential diversification into new business areas.
