To uninstall an app on macOS:

1. Open the Finder app on your Mac.
2. Click the Applications folder in the sidebar.
3. Find the app that you want to uninstall in the list of applications.
4. Drag the app icon to the Trash in the Dock.
5. Empty the Trash to uninstall the app.

Some apps have a dedicated uninstaller. Look for it in the /Applications folder. You can run the uninstaller to remove all of the files and settings of the app from your Mac.

Uninstalling an app does not always delete all of its associated files and data. To remove an app and all of its associated files, use a third-party app cleaner tool. You can also delete the remaining files manually.
