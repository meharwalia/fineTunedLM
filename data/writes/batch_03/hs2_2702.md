Use these tips to give great compliments:

1. **Be genuine.** Be sincere. A compliment from the heart is more meaningful than one that sounds forced or fake.
2. **Focus on inner beauty.** Compliment kindness, intelligence or sense of humor. This makes the person feel more comfortable and appreciated.
3. **Be specific.** A generic compliment such as "you look great today" can be nice. It can also sound generic. Point out something you like, such as their hair, outfit or smile.
4. **Avoid objectifying.** Do not make compliments that objectify or sexualize the person. They can make the person feel uncomfortable.
5. **Be respectful.** Always be respectful and polite. Avoid jokes or comments that people can consider inappropriate or offensive.
6. **Follow up with a question.** After the compliment, ask a question or make a comment. This shows that you want to know the person better.

Sincere and respectful compliments, with a focus on inner beauty and specific details, make the person feel appreciated and valued.
