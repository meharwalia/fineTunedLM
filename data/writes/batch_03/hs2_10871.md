A 0th order tensor is a scalar. In mathematics, it is a quantity that does not depend on any coordinate system or basis. It is a single number with no indices or components.

A scalar is a tensor of rank 0. The rank of a tensor is the number of indices that specify its components.

A vector is a tensor of rank 1. A matrix is a tensor of rank 2.

In tensor analysis, scalars are often building blocks for more complex tensors. The dot product of two vectors is a scalar. The norm of a vector is also a scalar.

In physics and engineering, scalars represent physical quantities such as mass, temperature and energy. In computer graphics and image processing, they represent pixel values and other image data.

0th order tensors are a fundamental concept in mathematics. They have applications in science and engineering.
