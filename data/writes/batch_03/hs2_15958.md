**Benefits of data analytics for credit scoring**

- **Accuracy:** Data analytics is often more accurate than other credit reporting methods.
- **Efficiency:** Data analytics is an automated credit scoring process. Scores arrive faster. This speeds up the lending process.
- **Fraud detection:** Data analytics makes fraud easy to detect. It catches abnormalities in credit applications.
- **Informed decisions:** Lenders assess the risks of each borrower. They can make more informed decisions.

**Challenges of data analytics for credit scoring**

- **Privacy:** Credit scoring collects a large amount of personal data. This raises privacy concerns.
- **Fairness:** Sometimes data analytics includes biased information in the data. This makes it difficult for marginalized groups to get fair reports.
- **Quality:** It is challenging to make sure that all of the collected data is of good quality. This has a significant impact on accuracy.
