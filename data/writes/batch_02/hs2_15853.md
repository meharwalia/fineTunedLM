According to Slate.com, nobody may ever know for certain. The first popular recording with profanity is probably Eddy Duchin's 1938 version of "Old Man Moses". The song is ripe with innuendo.

The published lyrics of the time include the word "bucket". This word stands in for the uttered phrase "f\*\*\* it".

As early as the 1920's, a genre known as "dirty blues" existed. It also contained much profanity. These recordings did not enter the mainstream. Nobody can prove that any of them was the "first" recording.
