Expect questions that test your ability to lead complex projects, your technical knowledge, and your understanding of the AR industry. Here are ten possible interview questions:

1. What experience do you have managing multi-disciplinary teams and complex projects?
2. What are the biggest challenges in developing AR goggles? How would you address them as a program manager?
3. How do you keep project timelines and budgets on target while you maintain high quality standards?
4. What role does opto-mechanics play in AR goggle development? How would you work with the opto-mechanical team to achieve project goals?
5. What do you know about current AR technologies and trends? How do you stay up to date with industry developments?
6. How do you handle conflicts within a team? What strategies do you use to promote collaboration and communication?
7. Can you give an example of a project that faced significant challenges? How did you overcome them to succeed?
8. How do you make sure the AR goggle development program aligns with the overall goals and objectives of the company?
9. What experience do you have working with regulatory bodies and getting certifications for consumer electronics products?
10. How would you approach launching a new AR goggle product? What strategies would you use to succeed in the market?
