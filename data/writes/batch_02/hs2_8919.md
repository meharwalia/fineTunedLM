Nordic Semiconductor handles the long tail of its go-to-market strategy with direct and indirect sales channels.

Nordic Semiconductor is a Norwegian company. It designs and markets wireless integrated circuits (ICs) for low power applications.

It focuses on the "Internet of Things" (IoT) market. This market includes smart home appliances, wearable devices, and other connected devices.

The company uses these sales channels:

- A direct sales force targets larger customers, such as major electronics manufacturers.
- Distributors and value-added resellers (VARs) reach a wider range of customers.

The company also offers tools and resources that help customers design and develop products. These include software, hardware, and technical support.

It also provides development boards and kits. These make it easier for customers to prototype and test devices.

Its approach gives customers a flexible and supportive environment. Its sales and support channels meet customer needs.
