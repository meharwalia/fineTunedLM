There are no significant biological differences between white and black people. Genetic variations may exist between these groups, but they do not cause meaningful biological differences.

Skin color depends on the amount of melanin, a pigment that the body produces. Skin color does not measure biological makeup.

Race is a social concept, not a biological one. Genetic differences may exist between populations. However, people have used the concept of race to categorize people and to create and maintain power imbalances and inequalities.

All people, regardless of race or ethnicity, belong to the same human species. They have more genetic similarities than differences. Any differences do not justify discrimination or inequality.
