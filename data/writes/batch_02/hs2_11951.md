Prosci certification shows that a person completed training in the Prosci ADKAR Model, a model for change management. Key points:

- Prosci, a leading provider of change management training and resources, offers the certification.
- To get certified, a person must complete a Prosci change management training course and pass an exam. The course covers the Prosci ADKAR Model, change management principles, and best practices for implementing change.
- The Prosci ADKAR Model is a five-step model for managing change. It focuses on building awareness, knowledge, ability, and reinforcement to achieve successful change.
- Prosci certification is a mark of expertise in change management. It can help people advance their careers. It can also improve their ability to lead and manage change initiatives in their organizations.
- The certification is valuable for project managers, HR professionals, business analysts, and organizational development professionals. It is also useful for leaders and managers who lead change initiatives in their organizations.
- The certification gives people a framework for understanding and managing change. It also gives them tools and techniques to implement change effectively. It can help them build their change management skills and knowledge. It also connects them with a global network of change management professionals.
