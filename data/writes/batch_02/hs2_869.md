No single brand is clearly best. Consider these factors when you choose a battery brand for a 5kW Sunsynk inverter:

- Battery type
- Capacity
- Cycle life
- Depth of discharge
- Warranty

Popular battery brands that work with Sunsynk inverters include Tesla, LG Chem, and Sonnen. Choose a brand that is reliable, has a good reputation, and offers a long warranty. Consult a professional solar installer. The installer can find the best battery brand and size for your energy needs and budget.
