To create a server (called a "Droplet" in DigitalOcean), follow these steps:

1. **Create a DigitalOcean account.** If you do not have one, sign up at <https://www.digitalocean.com/>.
2. **Log in.** Log in to the DigitalOcean Control Panel.
3. **Create a new Droplet.** Click the green "Create" button in the top right corner of the Control Panel. Then select "Droplets".
4. **Choose an image.** The image is the base operating system or application that your Droplet runs. Choose a Linux distribution, or select a pre-configured "One-Click" app.
5. **Choose a plan.** The plan sets the memory, storage, and CPU power of your Droplet. Choose the plan that fits your needs and budget.
6. **Add backups (optional).** DigitalOcean offers backups for an additional fee. Backups are copies of your Droplet that DigitalOcean takes at regular intervals. You can use them to restore your Droplet if something goes wrong.
7. **Choose a datacenter region.** This is the physical location that hosts your Droplet. Choose a region that is geographically close to your users to reduce latency.
8. **Add SSH keys (optional).** Add your SSH keys during Droplet creation to connect securely to your Droplet with SSH. This step is optional. It is highly recommended for security.
9. **Finalize and create.** Choose a hostname for your Droplet. Click the "Create Droplet" button. Your Droplet is ready to use in a few minutes.

After you create the Droplet, connect to it with SSH. Use a tool such as PuTTY (on Windows) or the terminal (on Mac or Linux). DigitalOcean emails you the connection details, including the IP address and root password. The details are also in the DigitalOcean Control Panel.
