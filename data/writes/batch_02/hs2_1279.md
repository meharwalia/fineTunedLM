Here are five backstory ideas for a 5e Aasimar fighter:

1. Aasimar of the Heavens: The fighter was born on a distant planet in the celestial realm. The gods sent the fighter to Earth to defeat an evil force that threatens the balance of the universe.
2. Fallen Angel: The fighter was once a powerful angel. A dark power corrupted the angel, who fell from grace. The celestial realm cast the fighter out and sent the fighter to Earth to atone for past sins. The fighter uses fighting skills to protect the innocent and restore lost honor.
3. The Last Hope: The fighter is the last surviving member of an ancient Aasimar civilization that once ruled the skies. The fighter must protect a powerful artifact. The artifact holds the key to restore the people to their former glory. The fighter will stop at nothing to fulfill this destiny.
4. The Redemption: The fighter was once a ruthless warrior who terrorized the skies. After a fateful encounter with a wise sage, the fighter saw the error of these ways. The fighter now dedicates life to protecting the innocent and bringing peace to the world.
5. The Guardian: The fighter was born with a divine purpose to protect a sacred site from anyone who would harm it. The fighter must keep the site safe from evil forces and will fight to the death to fulfill this duty.

These backstories give a rich context for a fighter character. You can develop them further as the campaign progresses.
