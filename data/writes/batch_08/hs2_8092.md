Giovanni was a young boy who loved to ride his bike. He rode all over his neighborhood. He explored new streets and met new friends.

One day, he decided to ride without his helmet. He did not think it was necessary. He wanted to feel the wind in his hair.

Giovanni rode down the hill by his middle school. He felt a rush of excitement. He went faster than ever before.

At the bottom of the hill, he hit a bump. The bump sent him flying over the handlebars. He landed with a thud and felt a sharp pain in his head.

His friends rushed to help him. They called an ambulance. It took Giovanni to the hospital.

The doctors examined him and found a cracked skull. They told his parents that he was lucky to be alive.

Giovanni was devastated. He had never been hurt before. He felt that he had let his parents down.

His parents were proud of him for being so brave. They told him that they loved him no matter what. They said they would always be there for him.

Giovanni learned his lesson the hard way. He never rode his bike without a helmet again. He always wore his seatbelt in the car.

He knew that he was lucky to be alive. He was grateful to the people who helped him.

Years later, Giovanni became a doctor. He wanted to help other people as people had helped him.

He knew that life can be unpredictable. He also knew that it was important to be prepared.

The End.
