To make a technology video, you need six things:

1. **A topic or idea:** For example, a product review, a how-to guide, or a spotlight on a technology.
2. **Equipment:** You need a camera or smartphone camera and editing software. A microphone is optional.
3. **A plan:** Decide the structure of the video, the information to include, and how to present it.
4. **Footage:** Film the footage. Capture clear and stable images. For a product review, film close-ups of the product and its features. For a how-to guide, film each step clearly.
5. **Editing:** Use editing software to put the footage together. Add text, music, and other effects as needed. Make sure the video flows logically and presents the information clearly.
6. **Publishing:** Upload the finished video to a video-sharing platform such as YouTube or Vimeo. Share it on social media and other relevant online communities.

A good technology video is informative, engaging, and visually appealing. With practice and patience, you can create high-quality technology videos that inform and inspire your audience.
