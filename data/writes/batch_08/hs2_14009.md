Monogamy is a relationship in which a person has only one partner at a time. It is a form of marriage in which one person is married to only one other person at a time.

It is the most common form of marriage in the world. Most governments recognize only this form of marriage.

People often see monogamy as a symbol of commitment and fidelity. People believe it benefits the emotional and physical health of both partners. It can also give a sense of security, stability, and belonging.
