Computer science uses statistics to design and analyze algorithms and systems. It also uses statistics to extract insights from data. Here are six examples:

1. **Machine Learning:** Statistics is the theoretical foundation of machine learning algorithms. It helps you understand their performance, select models, and evaluate results.
2. **Data Mining:** Statistics identifies patterns and trends in large datasets. Descriptive statistics summarize the data. Inferential statistics make predictions. Hypothesis testing validates findings.
3. **Natural Language Processing:** Statistics helps analyze and understand human language. Techniques include topic modeling, sentiment analysis, and word embeddings.
4. **Computer Vision:** Statistics helps analyze and interpret visual data. Techniques include object detection, image segmentation, and image recognition.
5. **Network Analysis:** Statistics helps explain the structure and behavior of complex networks. Techniques include graph theory, centrality measures, and community detection.
6. **Performance Evaluation:** Statistics helps assess the efficiency and effectiveness of computer systems and algorithms. Techniques include statistical testing, benchmarking, and modeling.

Statistics gives computer science the tools to analyze and interpret data, make predictions, and evaluate performance.
