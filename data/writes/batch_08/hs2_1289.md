You can gain weight in a healthy way with six steps:

1. **Increase calorie intake.** To gain weight, eat more calories than you burn. Add 300-500 calories to your daily diet. Choose calorie-dense foods such as nuts, nut butter, avocados, olive oil, and full-fat dairy products.
2. **Eat more protein.** Protein builds and repairs muscles. Eat protein-rich foods such as lean meats, fish, eggs, beans, and lentils.
3. **Eat healthy fats.** Healthy fats are essential for weight gain and overall health. Eat olive oil, avocados, nuts, and seeds.
4. **Eat more frequently.** Small meals more often help you eat more calories during the day. Eat every 3-4 hours to keep your metabolism active.
5. **Lift weights.** Resistance training builds muscle, which is denser than fat. Strength training helps you gain weight in a healthy way.
6. **Track your progress.** Keep a food diary of your calorie and nutrient intake. It helps you adjust your diet. It also helps you make sure that you eat enough calories to gain weight.

Gaining weight healthily takes time and patience. Avoid crash diets or fad diets that promise quick results.

They are unhealthy and unsustainable. Consult a registered dietitian or healthcare provider if you have concerns about your weight or nutrition.
