To convert a Miro flow chart into a written process, follow these steps:

1. **Analyze the flow chart.** Study the flow chart to understand the process. Identify the start and end points, the steps, and the decision points between them.
2. **Create an outline.** Use the information from the flow chart. Include the main steps and any sub-steps or decision points.
3. **Write the process.** Use the outline as a guide. Write the process clearly and concisely. Include all steps and decision points, and any relevant information or details.
4. **Review and revise.** Check that the written process accurately reflects the flow chart. Make the necessary revisions. Make sure the process is clear and easy to follow.
5. **Test the process.** Follow the process step by step to make sure it works as intended. Make additional revisions as needed.

These steps give you a written process that others can easily understand and follow.
