You can start to make money online in seven ways:

1. **Online surveys:** Get paid for your opinions. Companies look for consumer feedback and pay for it.
2. **Freelance work:** Offer your skills in writing, design, programming, and other areas. Find freelance work on websites such as Upwork, Fiverr, and Freelancer.
3. **Online retail:** Sell products on websites such as Amazon or eBay. Source products from suppliers or sell your own products.
4. **Affiliate marketing:** Promote the products of other people. Earn a commission for each sale you generate. Find affiliate programs on websites such as Commission Junction and Clickbank.
5. **Online courses:** Create and sell online courses on topics you know well. Use platforms such as Udemy and Coursera to reach a large audience.
6. **Online advertising:** Display ads on your website or blog. You earn money each time a person clicks an ad. Sign up for ad networks such as Google AdSense and Media.net.
7. **Virtual assistant:** Offer virtual assistant services to individuals or businesses. Tasks include scheduling appointments, managing email, and providing customer service.

Research each option. Find the option that suits your skills and interests.
