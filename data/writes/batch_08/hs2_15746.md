Introverts often feel drained from socializing. They feel comfortable when they are more reserved. These five ways help introverts feel more comfortable in social situations:

1. **Give them time alone.** After a social situation, introverts need time alone to recharge. If an introvert feels uncomfortable, give them time alone to collect themselves.
2. **Let them set the pace.** Introverts often feel overwhelmed when they socialize. They set the pace for conversation. Let them take the lead. Add to the conversation only when they start it.
3. **Avoid large groups.** Large groups overwhelm introverts. Limit the number of people to a number they can handle.
4. **Give them an "out".** If an introvert feels overwhelmed, give them an "out" from the social situation. Tell them they can leave early. Or give them a task that requires them to leave the gathering.
5. **Be understanding.** Introverts feel drained from socializing. Others do not always see their discomfort. Respect their needs. Do not push them to socialize if they are uncomfortable.
