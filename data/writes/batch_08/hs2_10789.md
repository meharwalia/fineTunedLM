Here are seven activities that help college students understand the limitations and possibilities of Chat GPT and AI in learning and productivity:

1. **AI-powered Research:** Students use Chat GPT and other AI tools to research a topic. They compare the results to traditional research methods. They evaluate the accuracy, relevance, and comprehensiveness of the AI results. They discuss the advantages and limitations of AI for research.
2. **AI-powered Content Creation:** Students use Chat GPT and other AI tools to create content, such as a blog post or a paper. They compare the AI content to their own work. They discuss the potential of AI to help with content creation.
3. **AI-powered Language Translation:** Students use Chat GPT and other AI tools to translate a text from one language to another. They compare the results to human translation. They discuss the accuracy and limitations of AI translation.
4. **AI-powered Creativity:** Students use Chat GPT and other AI tools to generate creative work, such as poetry or art. They discuss the potential of AI to inspire and help with creative work.
5. **AI-powered Problem Solving:** Students use Chat GPT and other AI tools to solve problems. They discuss the potential of AI to help with problem-solving in different fields.
6. **AI Ethics and Bias:** Students discuss the ethical considerations of AI, such as bias and fairness. They explore ways to reduce these issues.
7. **AI and Human Collaboration:** Students work in teams. Some team members use AI tools and others do not. The teams complete a task or project. They discuss the benefits and challenges of collaboration with AI.

These activities help students understand the capabilities and limitations of Chat GPT and AI. They also show the potential of AI to improve learning and productivity. Students develop a deeper understanding of the technology and its implications for different fields.
