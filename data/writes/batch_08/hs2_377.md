**One Step More**

This principle tells employees to strive for excellence and go the extra mile. Examples:

- A software engineer spends extra time to research and learn new technologies that can improve the company's products.
- A data scientist spends extra time to clean and preprocess data. This gives the highest quality for their models.
- A product manager spends extra time to understand customer needs and requirements. This makes sure the products meet customer expectations.

**Personal Growth**

This principle tells employees to learn continuously and develop their skills to reach their full potential. Examples:

- An employee takes online courses or attends conferences to learn new skills and knowledge.
- An employee asks colleagues and managers for feedback and constructive criticism to improve performance.
- An employee takes on new challenges and responsibilities to expand their experience and expertise.

**Sharing**

This principle tells employees to share their knowledge and experience with others. This helps the company achieve its goals. Examples:

- An employee mentors a junior colleague and helps them develop skills and knowledge.
- An employee joins knowledge sharing sessions and presents their work to colleagues.
- An employee collaborates with other teams and shares expertise to help the company achieve its objectives.

**One Team**

This principle tells employees to work together as a team to achieve the company's goals. Examples:

- An employee helps a colleague who struggles with a task or project.
- An employee joins team-building activities and events to build a positive team spirit.
- An employee communicates effectively with colleagues and collaborates with them to achieve common goals.

**Ethics**

This principle tells employees to act ethically and responsibly in all their actions and decisions. Examples:

- An employee refuses to compromise on ethical standards, even if it means losing a customer or missing a deadline.
- An employee reports unethical behavior or practices in the company.
- An employee takes responsibility for their actions and admits their mistakes.
