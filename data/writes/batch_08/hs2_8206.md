To generate a shadow from a 3D building model, use a ray tracing algorithm. Follow these steps:

1. Create a 3D model of the building with modeling software such as Blender, Maya, or 3ds Max.
2. Import the model into ray tracing software such as POV-Ray or Blender Render.
3. Create a scene with the building and other objects that cast a shadow, such as the sun or a light source.
4. Trace the light rays from the sun or light source to the building and other objects.
5. Calculate the angle and the position of the shadow on the ground or other surfaces.
6. Render the scene, including the shadow. Save the image.

The complexity of the scene and the quality of the 3D model affect the accuracy of the shadow. The capabilities of the ray tracing software also affect it.
