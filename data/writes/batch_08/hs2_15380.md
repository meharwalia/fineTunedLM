**Design 1: Underground Base**

1. Find a remote location with few players around.
2. Dig a 5x5 or 7x7 square hole. Go down at least 10 blocks.
3. Create a hidden entrance with a piston door or a hidden ladder system.
4. Build your base inside the hole. Make sure it is well-lit and secure.
5. Use iron doors and pressure plates for added security.
6. Create a farm, a storage system, and other necessary facilities.
7. Surround your base with traps, such as lava or water, to deter intruders.

**Design 3: Mountain Base**

1. Find a large mountain in a remote location.
2. Carve out a hidden entrance and interior space inside the mountain.
3. Build your base inside the mountain. Make sure it is well-lit and secure.
4. Use iron doors and pressure plates for added security.
5. Create a farm, a storage system, and other necessary facilities.
6. Surround your base with traps, such as lava or water, to deter intruders.

Stay cautious and aware of your surroundings, even in a hidden base. Keep your valuables hidden and secure. Do not share the location of your base with others.
