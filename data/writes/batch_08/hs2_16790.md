World Fairs are ongoing events. The most recent one took place in Dubai in 2022. In modern years, the event is called "World Expo".

The fair can seem to have disappeared because the United States has not hosted one since 1984. The Americans said the financial cost of the event was not justifiable. The United States still takes part in World Expos in other locations.

**World Expos from 1992 to 2022**

1. Seville, Spain - 1992
2. Hanover, Germany - 2000
3. Aichi, Japan - 2005
4. Shanghai, China - 2010
5. Milan, Italy - 2015
6. Dubai, United Arab Emirates - 2021-2022

**Notable remnants of past World Fairs**

- The Parthenon Replica in Nashville, Tennessee (1897)
- The Space Needle in Seattle, Washington (1962)
- The Unisphere in New York City, New York (1964)

These are not the only remnants.

**Facts about World Fairs**

1. The Small World ride at Disneyland first appeared at the 1964 World Expo in New York City.
2. Ice cream, color TV, diet soda, and Juicy Fruit gum are examples of items that started at a World Expo. Other items also started there.
3. Albert Einstein spoke at the 1879 World Expo in New York.
4. The 1893 World Fair in Chicago inspired Frank L. Baum to write *The Wizard of Oz*, according to reports.
5. England held the first World Fair in 1851.
6. Charlotte Bronte, Charles Dickens, and Lewis Carroll attended the first World Fair, "The Great Exposition".
7. The 1939 World Fair had a time capsule. It is for opening in 6939.
