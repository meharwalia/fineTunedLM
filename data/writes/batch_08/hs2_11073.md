This section describes the design of the Hazard Prediction Module (HPM) in the Data Fusion Cell (DFC) of the CBRN system. The HPM predicts the location and movement of CBRN hazards and contamination. It generates a continuously updated CBRN picture for other components of the system.

**6.1 General Description**

The HPM gives essential information on the location and movement of CBRN hazards. This information supports decisions and response actions. The HPM takes data from sensor networks, weather forecasts, and human reports.

It uses advanced algorithms and modeling techniques to analyze the data. It generates the most accurate CBRN picture possible.

**6.2 Design**

The HPM design aims to align the internal interfaces in the DFC. It keeps full fidelity of all relevant CBRN information. This supports accurate and timely CBRN hazard prediction.

The design also makes the CBRN picture continuously available to other components of the DFC. This supports decisions and response actions.

**6.3 Technical Description**

The HPM uses advanced algorithms and modeling techniques to analyze data and predict the location and movement of CBRN hazards and contamination. It has four main components:

- **Data Acquisition:** This component collects data from sensor networks, weather forecasts, and human reports. The data includes the location and extent of CBRN hazards, weather conditions, and other relevant information.
- **Data Pre-processing:** This component prepares the collected data for analysis. It cleans and formats the data. It also transforms the data into a form the analysis component can use.
- **Analysis:** This component analyzes the pre-processed data and generates the CBRN picture. It does pattern recognition, data fusion, and cloud movement prediction.
- **Output Generation:** This component generates the CBRN picture in a format that other components of the DFC can use. The picture includes the location and movement of CBRN hazards and contamination. It also includes weather conditions and predicted cloud movement.

**6.4 Responsibilities**

The HPM has five main responsibilities:

- Predict the location and movement of CBRN hazards and contamination.
- Generate a continuously updated CBRN picture for other components of the DFC.
- Analyze data from different sources to generate the most accurate CBRN picture possible.
- Pre-process the collected data for analysis.
- Generate the CBRN picture and distribute it to other components of the DFC.
