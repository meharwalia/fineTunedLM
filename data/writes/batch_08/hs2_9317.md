Yes, you can configure the 3 axes so that the image always points "north up". Use a combination of sensors and control algorithms.

They continuously adjust the orientation of the camera. The adjustment is based on the movement of the aircraft and the position of the point on the ground.

- **Azimuth axis:** Rotates the camera around the vertical axis. This keeps a constant orientation relative to the ground.
- **Roll axis:** Compensates for roll movements of the aircraft. This keeps the camera level.
- **Elevation axis:** Adjusts the angle of view. This keeps a constant view of the point on the ground.

With these three axes and real-time feedback from sensors, you create a stable and accurate image that always points "north up". Aerial surveillance, mapping, and other applications that need precise camera orientation commonly use this type of system.
