To perform a situational analysis of a solid and liquid waste management scheme, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the stakeholders.** Find the key stakeholders. They include the local community, government agencies, waste management companies, and environmental organizations.
2. **Assess the current situation.** Evaluate the current solid and liquid waste management practices in the area. Include the types of waste, the collection and disposal methods, and the regulations and policies.
3. **Analyze the strengths and weaknesses.** Assess the strengths and weaknesses of the scheme. Include the infrastructure, resources, and personnel. Include the challenges or obstacles that affect its effectiveness.
4. **Identify opportunities and threats.** Find opportunities for improvement and potential threats. Examples are changes in the local community, new technologies, or changes in regulations.
5. **Set goals and objectives.** Establish clear goals and objectives. Examples are less waste generation, higher recycling rates, or better waste disposal methods.
6. **Develop a plan of action.** The plan can include new technologies, education of the community, or partnerships with other organizations.
7. **Monitor and evaluate the results.** Monitor and evaluate the results regularly. Make sure that the scheme meets its goals and objectives. Make adjustments as needed.

These steps give you a situational analysis and a plan to improve the effectiveness and sustainability of the scheme.
