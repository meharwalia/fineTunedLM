You can help your friend if you work to resolve the fight. Try these four steps:

1. **Take responsibility for your part.** Admit your mistakes and apologize if necessary. This builds trust. It shows that you want a resolution.
2. **Communicate openly and honestly.** Tell your friend how their involvement in the conflict makes you feel. State your expectations clearly. Listen to their perspective.
3. **Compromise.** Look for a solution that works for both of you. You can make sacrifices or adjust your original plans.
4. **Seek outside help.** If necessary, ask a neutral third party to help the conversation. This can be a mutual friend, a therapist, or a mediator.

Put the well-being of your friends first. Treat them with respect and kindness, even in difficult times.
