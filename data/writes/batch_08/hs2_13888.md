A commonly accepted range for plug loads in an office space is 10 to 20 W/sf. The actual value depends on the type of equipment, the number of people, and the level of activity.

These are general guidelines for load calculations:

- **Lighting:** Start with 1 to 2 W/sf. The load depends on the type of lighting and the number of people.
- **Computer equipment:** Start with 5 to 10 W/sf. The load depends on the type of equipment and the number of people who use it.
- **Office equipment:** Start with 1 to 5 W/sf. The load depends on the type of equipment and the number of people who use it.
- **HVAC system:** Start with 15 to 25 W/sf. The load depends on the type of system and the climate of the building.

These are general guidelines. The actual plug loads can change with the needs of the office space. Consult a professional HVAC engineer or energy consultant to calculate the plug loads for your office space.
