These standard operating procedures (SOPs) apply to both office work and software development:

- **Regular backups:** Keep a regular backup routine. You can restore all important data after a data loss.
- **Version control:** Use a version control system, such as Git, in software development. It tracks changes to the codebase and lets team members collaborate.
- **Issue tracking:** Use an issue tracking system. It tracks tasks, bugs, and other issues that need attention.
- **Communication:** Hold regular team meetings and status updates. Everyone then stays informed.
- **Security:** Use strong security measures. They protect against unauthorized access, data breaches, and other security threats.
- **Training:** Give regular training and development. Employees then stay current with new technologies, processes, and best practices.
- **Quality assurance:** Use a quality assurance process. It makes sure all work is of a high standard and meets the required specifications.
- **Incident response:** Keep an incident response plan. It helps you deal with unexpected events.
- **Change management:** Use a change management process. It makes sure that changes are controlled and coordinated.
