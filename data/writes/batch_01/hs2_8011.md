Follow these tips to take care of orchids:

- **Light:** Give orchids bright, indirect sunlight. Direct sunlight can burn the leaves.
- **Water:** Water them regularly, but do not overwater them. Let the soil dry out slightly between waterings.
- **Fertilizer:** Use a balanced fertilizer once a month during the growing season.
- **Humidity:** Orchids prefer high humidity. They can also adapt to normal household humidity levels. To increase humidity, put the orchid on a tray of wet gravel or use a humidifier.
- **Repotting:** Repot orchids every 2-3 years, or when they become root-bound. Use a well-draining orchid potting mix.
- **Pests and diseases:** Inspect the orchid regularly for pests and diseases. Treat any problems promptly.
- **Special conditions:** Some orchids need special conditions. Research the specific type of orchid you have.

With proper care, orchids can bloom repeatedly for years.
