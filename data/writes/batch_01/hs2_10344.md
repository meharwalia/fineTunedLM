These are 15 music composition techniques:

1. **Motif/Melody:** Develop and repeat a short musical idea, or motif, through a piece to create a melody.
2. **Harmony:** Play multiple notes at the same time to create chords and chord progressions.
3. **Counterpoint:** Combine two or more melodic lines that are independent but harmonically interdependent.
4. **Form:** The structure of a piece, such as sonata form, rondo form or binary form.
5. **Timbre:** The unique sound or color of an instrument or voice. Composers use timbre to create contrast and interest.
6. **Texture:** The thickness or thinness of a piece. The number of voices or instruments that play at the same time creates it.
7. **Dynamics:** Use volume to create contrast and emotion.
8. **Rhythm:** Use patterns of sound and silence to create a sense of pulse and drive.
9. **Repetition:** Repeat musical ideas to create a sense of familiarity and structure.
10. **Variation:** Change musical ideas to create interest and development.
11. **Improvisation:** Compose on the spot. Jazz and other forms of contemporary music often use it.
12. **Serialism:** Follow a specific pattern of notes through a piece.
13. **Minimalism:** A style of music that uses repetition and simplicity to create a hypnotic effect.
14. **Electronic music composition:** Use electronic technology to create and change sound.
15. **Aleatoric music:** Leave some elements of the composition to chance. This allows unexpected and unique outcomes.
