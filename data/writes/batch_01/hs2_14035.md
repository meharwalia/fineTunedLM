You have three options for inference logging in a FastAPI application deployed on Kubernetes (k8s):

1. **Use a logging library.** Use a library such as `logging` or `loguru` to log inference requests and responses. Set the logging level, format and output destination as you need. The destination can be the console, a file or a cloud logging service.
2. **Use a distributed tracing system.** Use a system such as `Jaeger` or `Zipkin`. It traces inference requests and responses across multiple services and components in your k8s cluster. This helps you find performance bottlenecks and troubleshoot issues.
3. **Use a monitoring and observability platform.** Use a platform such as `Datadog` or `New Relic`. It collects and analyzes inference metrics, logs and traces. It gives real-time insights into the performance of your application. It helps you find and resolve issues quickly.

With any option, make sure that your logging and monitoring setup is secure. It must comply with all relevant regulations or policies.

Also consider the performance impact of logging and monitoring on your application. Adjust your configuration for it.
