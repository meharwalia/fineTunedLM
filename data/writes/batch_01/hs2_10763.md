This is a pre-assessment for a Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) on the Essential 8. Answer the questions in each area.

**1. Application Control**

- Do you have a list of approved applications that can run on your systems?
- Do you use software restriction policies or application whitelisting to stop unauthorized applications?
- Do you review your list of approved applications at regular intervals?

**2. Patch Applications**

- Do you have a process to patch all applications at regular intervals, including third-party software?
- Do you test patches before you deploy them? This checks for compatibility issues or other problems.
- Do you have a system for the patch status of all applications?

**3. Configure Microsoft Office Macro Settings**

- Have you disabled macros in Microsoft Office applications by default?
- Do you have a process to review and approve macros before you enable them?
- Do you train users on the risks of enabling macros? Do you train them to identify potentially malicious macros?

**4. User Application Hardening**

- Do you use user application hardening measures? Examples are removing administrative rights from user accounts and using least privilege principles.
- Do you review these measures at regular intervals?
- Do you train users on the importance of user application hardening? Do you train them to work within the constraints of these measures?

**5. Restrict Administrative Privileges**

- Do you give administrative privileges only to users who need them for their job functions?
- Do you review administrative privileges at regular intervals?
- Do you train users on the risks of administrative privileges? Do you train them to work within the constraints of these restrictions?

**6. Patch Operating Systems**

- Do you have a process to patch all operating systems at regular intervals, including servers and workstations?
- Do you test patches before you deploy them? This checks for compatibility issues or other problems.
- Do you have a system for the patch status of all operating systems?
