The Oculus Quest 2 is a standalone virtual reality headset. It succeeds the Oculus Quest 1.

Users play games and explore virtual worlds on the screen of the headset. They need no computer or phone. Compared to the original Quest, the Quest 2 has a higher resolution screen, more memory and a faster processor.

The Quest 2 uses "inside-out tracking." Cameras on the headset track the movements of users.

They also track the controllers in the hands of users, or the hands themselves. Users can move around freely. They can interact easily with the virtual environment.

The headset has built-in speakers and a microphone. Users hear sounds and talk to others online while they wear the headset.

The Quest 2 runs on the Android platform. It has a large library of games and applications that users download from the Oculus Store.

The library includes popular games like Beat Saber and Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge.
