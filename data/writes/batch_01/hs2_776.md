In a PSAO agreement with a PBM, the dispensing fee is part of the reimbursement calculation. PSAO means Pharmacy Services Administration Organization. PBM means Pharmacy Benefit Manager.

**Dispensing fee**

The dispensing fee is the amount the pharmacy gets for filling a prescription. It covers the cost of the medication, the time and labor to prepare the prescription, and other costs such as overhead and profit.

The PBM sets the dispensing fee in the PSAO agreement. It uses these factors:

- the type of medication
- the strength of the medication
- the dosage form
- the type of insurance coverage

The PBM or the insurance company usually pays the fee directly to the pharmacy. The fee is separate from the patient's copay or coinsurance.

**Reimbursement rate**

The PBM uses the dispensing fee to calculate the reimbursement rate. The reimbursement rate is the amount the PBM pays the pharmacy for the medication and the dispensing fee. The PBM sets it from the cost of the medication, the dispensing fee and the type of insurance coverage.

The reimbursement rate sets how much the pharmacy gets for each prescription. If the rate is low, the pharmacy may not cover its costs. It can then opt out of the PSAO agreement with the PBM.
