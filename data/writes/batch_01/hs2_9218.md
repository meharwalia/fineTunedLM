**Outline for a 5-minute presentation: "How to motivate employees"**

I. Introduction
- A. Explain why it is important to motivate employees.
- B. Give a brief overview of the key points of the presentation.

II. Understanding Motivation
- A. Explain what motivation is and its role in the workplace.
- B. Discuss the types of motivation: intrinsic and extrinsic.

III. Factors that Motivate Employees
- A. Autonomy: give employees freedom and control over their work.
- B. Mastery: create opportunities for employees to learn, grow and get better at what they do.
- C. Purpose: help employees understand how their work contributes to the mission and goals of the organization.

IV. Strategies for Motivating Employees
- A. Use open and transparent communication.
- B. Give regular feedback and recognition.
- C. Offer opportunities for professional development and growth.
- D. Set clear goals and expectations.
- E. Provide a positive and inclusive work environment.

V. Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- A. Relying only on financial incentives.
- B. Micromanaging and stifling autonomy.
- C. Failing to give regular feedback and recognition.

VI. Conclusion
- A. Recap the key takeaways.
- B. Encourage the audience to use these strategies in their own organizations.
- C. Give final thoughts on the importance of motivating employees for the success of any organization.
