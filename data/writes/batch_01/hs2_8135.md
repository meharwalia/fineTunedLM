As a streamer, you want a positive and inclusive environment for your viewers. To tell someone kindly not to backseat or give advice unless you ask:

1. **Set clear boundaries.** Tell your viewers what you expect. Write a set of rules or guidelines for your stream. Include a policy on backseat gaming and advice giving. Enforce the rules in a consistent and just way.
2. **Address the viewer directly.** Be polite. Thank the viewer for the input. Say that you have the stream under control. Thank the viewer for their understanding.
3. **Use a moderation tool.** Persistent backseat gamers or advice givers can be a problem. Use a moderation tool like Streamlabs or OBS to manage your chat. These tools can set up custom commands and filters. The filters remove or silence messages automatically.
4. **Encourage positive feedback.** Do not only tell viewers not to give advice or backseat game. Ask your viewers questions. Encourage discussion. Acknowledge and appreciate their comments and contributions.
5. **Be patient and understanding.** Viewers can not know your expectations. They can break a rule by accident. Use this as a chance to teach them the rules and guidelines of your stream.

With these five steps, you create a positive and inclusive environment. You also keep control of your stream.
