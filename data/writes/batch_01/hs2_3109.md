The steering wheel angle is not directly proportional to the actual wheel angle. The steering mechanism changes the rotation of the steering wheel into movement of the wheels.

The relationship between the two angles is complex. It depends on the type of steering system, the speed of the car and the road conditions.

You can estimate the actual wheel angle from the steering wheel angle with these methods:

- **Steering angle sensor:** The sensor measures the rotation of the steering wheel. It converts the rotation to an electrical signal. A mathematical model of the steering system then processes the signal to estimate the actual wheel angle.
- **Camera system:** The camera tracks the position of the wheels and the steering wheel. Image analysis estimates the wheel angle and the steering wheel angle. You then use this information to calculate the relationship between the two angles.

The conversion is not simple. You must consider the specific vehicle and the driving conditions.
