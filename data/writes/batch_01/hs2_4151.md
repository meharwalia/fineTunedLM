A kindergartner must learn foundational skills. These skills help the child succeed in school and in life. These are the five key areas:

1. **Reading and Language Arts:** Kindergartners develop reading skills, including phonemic awareness, phonics and vocabulary. They also develop writing skills, including letter formation, handwriting, and basic grammar and sentence structure.
2. **Mathematics:** Kindergartners develop mathematical skills, including number recognition, counting, addition and subtraction, and basic geometry and measurement.
3. **Social and Emotional Development:** Kindergartners learn to interact with others and develop social skills. They learn to manage their emotions, resolve conflicts and work together with others.
4. **Physical Development:** Kindergartners need opportunities to be physically active. They develop gross and fine motor skills. Running, jumping and playing games help. Drawing and cutting develop hand-eye coordination and dexterity.
5. **Creativity and Imagination:** Kindergartners need opportunities to explore their creativity and imagination through art, music and dramatic play. These activities help them develop problem-solving and critical thinking skills.

Learning must be fun and engaging for young children. Play-based learning and hands-on activities help kindergartners develop these skills.
