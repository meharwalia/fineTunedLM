No single TikTok ads strategy is best for a $59 dropshipping product with $39 profit per sale. The best strategy depends on the target audience, the product and the marketing budget. These general tips can help:

1. **Define your target audience.** Know who your target audience is before you create ads. Then you can tailor your ads to their interests and preferences. This makes a purchase more likely.
2. **Use eye-catching visuals.** TikTok is a highly visual platform. Use bright colors, bold text and engaging animations to make your ads stand out.
3. **Use popular trends and hashtags.** TikTok has viral trends and hashtags. Use them in your ads to increase their visibility and reach.
4. **Keep it short.** TikTok videos are short. Keep your ads concise. Highlight the key features and benefits of the product clearly.
5. **Test and optimize.** Test your ads to see what works best. Try different ad formats, targeting options and creative elements. Find out what your target audience likes most.

The key to success with TikTok ads for dropshipping is to be creative, strategic and data-driven. Test and optimize your ads continually. This increases your chances of success and profitable sales.
