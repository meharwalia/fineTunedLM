To enable Thrive on Higher Logic:

1. Go to the Higher Logic website. Log in to your account.
2. Click the "Communities" tab in the top menu bar.
3. Select the community that you want to enable Thrive for.
4. Click the "Settings" tab in the left sidebar.
5. Scroll down to the "Community Features" section. Select "Thrive" from the list of features.
6. Click the "Save Changes" button at the bottom of the page.

Thrive is now enabled for your community on Higher Logic.

Note: The changes can take a few hours to start. Wait if you do not see the Thrive feature immediately.
