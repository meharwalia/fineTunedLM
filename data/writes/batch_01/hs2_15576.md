Take these seven steps to protect your privacy on Windows:

1. **Turn off location services.** Windows can track your location. It can share this information with third-party applications and services. Turn off location services in the Settings app.
2. **Clear your browsing history.** Your web browser can store the websites you visit and your stored passwords. Clear this information at regular intervals.
3. **Use a VPN.** A virtual private network (VPN) encrypts your internet connections. It also hides your IP address.
4. **Use a password manager.** A password manager generates and stores strong, unique passwords for all your accounts.
5. **Set privacy settings.** Windows applications and services have privacy settings. Use them to control what information goes to third parties. Make sure they match your preferences.
6. **Be careful with installed software.** Do not download or install software from untrusted sources. These programs can collect your personal information and share it with third parties.
7. **Keep your operating system and software up to date.** Windows releases security updates for known vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates.

These steps reduce the risk that third parties get your personal information.
