This is a sample resignation letter for your friend:

Dear [Manager's Name],

I am resigning from my position as a Sr. Software Engineer in the Infrastructure & Platform group at General Motors. My last day with the company is April 20th, two months from now.

I appreciate the opportunity to work with such a talented team. I learned a lot during my time here.

Thank you for your guidance and support. I wish you all the best in the future.

I will do my best to make the transition smooth during my remaining time at General Motors. If I can do anything to make this process easier, please tell me.

Thank you again for everything. I hope we stay in touch.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
