A sustainability manager implements and manages sustainability initiatives in an organization. The job description changes with the industry and the organization. These are the common duties:

1. **Develop and implement sustainability strategies.** The strategies align with the goals and values of the organization. This can include research, data analysis and finding areas for improvement.
2. **Manage sustainability projects.** The manager runs projects from start to finish. This can include project plans, budgets and oversight of the implementation of initiatives.
3. **Monitor and report on sustainability performance.** This can include collecting and analyzing data, writing reports and telling stakeholders about progress.
4. **Collaborate with stakeholders.** The manager works closely with employees, management and suppliers across the organization. Together they promote and implement sustainability initiatives.
5. **Conduct sustainability audits.** Audits find areas for improvement. They also make sure the organization complies with sustainability standards.
6. **Stay up to date with industry trends.** The manager keeps up with trends and best practices in sustainability. This can include conferences, workshops and training courses.
7. **Manage sustainability budgets.** The manager makes sure that sustainability initiatives are cost-effective.
8. **Communicate with the public.** The manager tells the public and stakeholders about the sustainability initiatives and progress of the organization.
9. **Develop and implement sustainability policies.** The policies and procedures promote sustainability in the organization.
10. **Manage sustainability suppliers.** The manager makes sure that suppliers meet the sustainability standards of the organization.

A sustainability manager has a critical role in the organization. The manager works to reduce the environmental impact of the organization. The manager also works to improve efficiency and promote sustainability practices throughout the organization.
