The quote is a prophecy from the Harry Potter series. It describes the relationship between Harry Potter and Lord Voldemort. One of them must kill the other for either to survive.

In Counter-Strike 1.6, the quote works as a metaphor for the relationship between the terrorists and the counter-terrorists. Harry and Voldemort fight a battle to the death. The two teams fight a constant struggle for control of the map.

The two teams have different objectives:

- The terrorists plant a bomb and eliminate the counter-terrorists.
- The counter-terrorists stop the bomb plant. They defuse the bomb if the terrorists plant it.

Harry and Voldemort cannot coexist. The two teams cannot coexist in the game.

One team must eliminate the other to reach its objective. The game has only one winner. The losing team must be completely eliminated.

The quote is a metaphor for the zero-sum nature of the conflict between the terrorists and the counter-terrorists in Counter-Strike 1.6.
