Yes, I know the Build-Measure-Learn framework. It is a core component of the Lean Startup methodology from Eric Ries. The framework is a loop.

It helps startups and businesses create, test and validate their ideas in a systematic and efficient way.

1. **Build:** Create a minimum viable product (MVP) or a prototype of the product or service. Test it with potential customers to gather feedback.
2. **Measure:** Collect data and feedback from the MVP or prototype. Use surveys, interviews or analytics tools. Use the data to measure the success of the product or service. Use it to find areas for improvement.
3. **Learn:** Analyze the data and feedback from the Measure stage. Learn from the results. Make informed decisions about the product or service.

In the Learn stage, you have three options:

- Pivot to a new direction.
- Iterate on the current product.
- Continue to build and test the product.

The framework is a continuous process. It helps businesses validate their ideas, reduce risk and increase their chances of success.

It is a powerful tool for startups and businesses of all sizes.
