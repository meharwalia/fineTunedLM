At Brigham and Women's Hospital (BWH) in Boston, Massachusetts, patients can ask for a second opinion from another physician at any time. The hospital encourages patients to take an active part in their healthcare and to make informed decisions. To get a second opinion at BWH, do these general steps:

1. Talk to your primary care physician or specialist about a second opinion. They can give you a referral or recommend other specialists in the BWH network.
2. Contact the BWH physician's office you want to see. You or your doctor can call the office directly. You can also call the BWH Patient Access Center at 1-800-294-9999 (toll-free). Prepare your medical history and relevant test results.
3. Schedule the appointment. The physician's office helps you find a convenient time. Some specialists have long wait times, so plan ahead.
4. Give the medical records. The office can ask you to bring or send your medical records, imaging studies and test results before the consultation. It tells you the specific requirements when you schedule the appointment.
5. Go to the consultation. Bring a list of questions or concerns. The second opinion physician reviews your medical history and examines you. The physician can recommend more tests or procedures.
6. Discuss the next steps with your primary care physician or specialist. Share the findings and recommendations with your original doctor. Together, decide the best course of action for your health.

Your insurance plan can have specific requirements or restrictions for second opinions.

Check your coverage and find out the out-of-pocket costs. BWH can guide you through the process. You are responsible for compliance with your insurance plan.
