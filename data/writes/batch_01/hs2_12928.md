This R code updates the model `res`. It removes the term `radio` and adds an offset term of `0.200*radio`.

- The `update` function modifies an existing model.
- The `. ~ .` syntax shows which terms stay in the model.
- The `- radio` part removes the term `radio`.
- The `offset(0.200*radio)` part adds a new offset term. This term equals `0.200` times the value of `radio`.

The offset term adjusts the intercept of the model. This helps when the predictor variable has a high correlation with the response variable.
