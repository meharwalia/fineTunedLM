Acidic and alkaline foods differ in pH level. The pH level measures how acidic or basic a substance is.

**Acidic foods**

- They have a pH level below 7.0.
- They include citrus fruits, tomatoes, vinegar and coffee.
- They are more common in our diet than alkaline foods.

**Alkaline foods**

- They have a pH level above 7.0.
- They include green leafy vegetables, avocados, bananas and nuts.
- They are less common in our diet than acidic foods.

**Which is better**

The human body is slightly alkaline, with a pH of about 7.35 to 7.45. Experts debate the effects of acidic and alkaline foods on the body. An alkaline diet has these potential benefits:

- It may reduce the risk of chronic diseases such as osteoporosis, heart disease and cancer.
- It can improve digestion and absorption of nutrients.
- It may boost the immune system and increase energy levels.

There is limited scientific evidence for these claims. A balanced diet with foods from all food groups is generally the best approach for good health.
