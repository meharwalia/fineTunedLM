These are the pros and cons of a Plex server and a Netflix subscription.

**Pros of a Plex server**

- You have full control over your media library. You can customize it.
- You can access your media library from anywhere in the world with an internet connection.
- You can share your media library with friends and family who have a Plex account.
- You can add plugins to extend Plex. For example, a plugin can add support for streaming from other services such as YouTube or Vimeo.

**Cons of a Plex server**

- You need a computer or device that is always on and connected to the internet to host the server.
- You need a good internet connection to stream media from the server to your devices.
- You need technical knowledge to set up and maintain the server. This includes configuring ports and setting up sharing permissions.

**Pros of Netflix**

- You can stream a large library of movies and TV shows on demand.
- Netflix is easy to use. You need no technical knowledge to set it up or maintain it.
- Netflix is available on a wide range of devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops and smart TVs.

**Cons of Netflix**

- You are limited to the movies and TV shows on Netflix.
- You need a Netflix subscription, which costs money.
- You cannot share your Netflix account with friends and family who do not live in your household.

The choice depends on your personal preferences and needs. If you value full control over your media library and the ability to share it, a Plex server can be the better option. If you value convenience and ease of use, Netflix can be the better choice.
