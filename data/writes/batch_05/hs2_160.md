This is an outline for a strong personal statement for graduate school in the US:

1. **Introduction.** Start with a strong opening sentence. It captures the attention of the reader. It sets the tone for the statement.
2. **Background.** Give a brief overview of your educational and professional background. Include relevant experiences, skills, and accomplishments.
3. **Goals.** Explain why you want a graduate degree in the US. Explain how it aligns with your long-term career goals.
4. **Strengths.** Highlight your strengths and unique qualities. They make you a strong candidate for the program.
5. **Challenges.** Describe each challenge or obstacle in your academic or professional journey. Explain how you overcame it.
6. **Contributions.** Explain how you plan to contribute to the program and the wider community. Explain what you hope to gain from the experience.
7. **Conclusion.** Summarize your key points. Express your enthusiasm for the program and your future goals.

Keep the statement concise and clear. Focus on the program and your goals. Good luck with your application!
