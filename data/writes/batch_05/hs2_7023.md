A brag document shows your accomplishments, skills, and experiences. They make you a strong candidate for the senior web developer position. To write an effective brag document:

1. **Identify your accomplishments.** List your significant accomplishments as a web developer. Highlight each project that had a positive effect on the company, such as more website traffic or better user experience. Be specific. Give numbers or metrics that show your effect.
2. **Highlight your technical skills.** A senior web developer needs a strong technical skillset. List the programming languages, frameworks, and tools that you know well. Give examples of how you used them in your work.
3. **Show your leadership experience.** If you led teams or projects, highlight this. Describe your approach to leadership. Describe your ability to motivate and manage teams. Describe your successes in these roles.
4. **Discuss your education and certifications.** Include relevant education or certifications. They show your knowledge and expertise in the field.
5. **Give examples of your work.** If possible, include examples of your work as a web developer. These can be code samples, website designs, or other projects.
6. **Keep it professional.** Show your accomplishments and skills. Do not use boastful or arrogant language.
7. **Proofread your document.** Before you submit it, check it carefully for errors and typos. A well-written and polished document makes a strong impression on your employer.

A good brag document highlights your accomplishments, skills, and experiences. It shows your qualifications for the senior web developer position. It increases your chances of a promotion.
