A burger is a type of sandwich. A sandwich is a broader term.

- **Burger:** It has a cooked ground meat patty, usually beef, inside a bun or roll. The patty typically has salt and pepper. Toppings can include cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and condiments such as ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise.
- **Sandwich:** It is any dish where bread holds other ingredients together. Fillings can be meat, cheese, vegetables, and spreads. People serve it hot or cold.

The main difference is that a burger is a sandwich with a cooked ground meat patty. A sandwich is a general term. It includes a wide variety of fillings.

These examples show the difference:

- A cheeseburger is a burger with a cheese slice on top of the meat patty.
- A chicken sandwich is a sandwich with cooked chicken breast as the main filling.
- A BLT (bacon, lettuce, and tomato) sandwich has bacon, lettuce, and tomato as the main fillings.
- A veggie burger is a burger with a plant-based patty instead of meat.
