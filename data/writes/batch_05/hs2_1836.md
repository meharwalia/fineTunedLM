Hindutva and fascism have seven similarities:

1. **Authoritarianism.** Both believe that power belongs with a single authority, such as the state or the leader of the movement.
2. **Nationalism.** Both are highly nationalistic. They strongly emphasize the nation and its culture.
3. **Anti-pluralism.** Both reject a pluralistic society. They support a society with only one dominant group.
4. **Anti-egalitarianism.** Both reject equality. They believe that one group is superior to another.
5. **Use of violence.** Both have a history of violence to achieve their goals. This includes violence against political opponents and minority groups.
6. **Anti-democracy.** Both oppose democracy. They support a strong leader with absolute power.
7. **Xenophobia.** Both are often xenophobic. Xenophobia is fear and hatred of people who differ from the dominant group.

Not all forms of Hindutva are the same. Not all Hindutva supporters have the same beliefs. The same is true of fascism. But the two share similarities, especially in authoritarianism, nationalism, anti-pluralism, anti-egalitarianism, use of violence, and anti-democratic beliefs.
