Choose a number other than 8. I cannot predict the future or guarantee an outcome. But the number 8 came up in 4 of the last 6 draws. The concept of probability says that past occurrences influence the likelihood of an event.

Each draw is independent. The outcome of one draw does not affect the outcome of another. A number other than 8 can be a reasonable strategy. But the number 8 can still come up again.
