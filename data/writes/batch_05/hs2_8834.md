To verify the Ethereum wallet addresses of your website users, use a tool like Etherscan or MetaMask. Etherscan is a block explorer and analytics platform for the Ethereum blockchain. MetaMask is a popular Ethereum wallet and browser extension.

**Use Etherscan**

1. Go to Etherscan. Click the "Address" tab.
2. Enter the Ethereum wallet address to verify.
3. Click the "Verify" button.
4. Etherscan shows the balance of the wallet, the transaction history, and other information.

**Use MetaMask**

1. Install the MetaMask browser extension. Create a wallet.
2. Ask your users to install MetaMask. Ask them to create a wallet.
3. Ask your users to connect their MetaMask wallet to your website.
4. Use the MetaMask API to verify the wallet address that your user uses on your website.

Always take precautions with sensitive user data. Have a clear privacy policy.
