FASTag is a digital payment system for cashless payments at toll plazas in India. You can buy FASTag on these platforms:

1. **NETC website.** The National Electronic Toll Collection (NETC) website is the official FASTag platform. To buy FASTag there:
   1. Register on the website.
   2. Give your personal details and vehicle details.
   3. Select a bank.
   4. Pay for the FASTag.
2. **Bank websites.** Banks in India sell FASTag on their websites. Go to the website of your bank. Follow the steps to buy FASTag. Banks that offer FASTag include ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, SBI, and Paytm Payments Bank.
3. **E-commerce websites.** You can also buy FASTag on Amazon, Flipkart, and Paytm Mall. Search for FASTag. Select the product. Pay to complete the purchase.
4. **Toll plazas.** You can buy FASTag at toll plazas in India. Buy it in advance to avoid last-minute problems. At the toll plaza, give your vehicle details and pay.
5. **Mobile applications.** These apps sell FASTag: the Paytm app, the ICICI Bank iMobile app, and the HDFC Bank MobileBanking app. Download an app. Follow the steps to buy FASTag.

Before you buy FASTag, check these points:

- Your vehicle must be compatible with the FASTag system.
- You need a valid bank account.
- You need a registered mobile number.
