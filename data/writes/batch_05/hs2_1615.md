This is a recipe for bun thit nuong, a popular Vietnamese dish.

**Ingredients**

- 1 pound pork shoulder, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh mint
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
- 1/4 cup chopped shallots
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup water
- 8-10 rice noodles, cooked as the package instructions say
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 1/2 cup chopped roasted peanuts
- 1/4 cup lime wedges
- 1/4 cup nuoc mam cham dipping sauce (recipe below)

**Instructions**

1. In a large bowl, mix the fish sauce, garlic, brown sugar, and black pepper.
2. Add the pork cubes. Stir to coat them with the sauce.
3. Cover the bowl. Refrigerate it for at least 30 minutes, or up to 24 hours.
4. Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high heat.
5. Thread the pork cubes onto skewers.
6. Grill the pork for 5-7 minutes per side, or until it is cooked through.
7. While the pork grills, make the nuoc mam cham dipping sauce. Mix 1/4 cup fish sauce, 1/4 cup lime juice, 1/4 cup water, 2 tablespoons sugar, and 2 cloves minced garlic. Stir until the sugar dissolves. Put the sauce aside.
8. In a large serving bowl, mix the cooked noodles, bean sprouts, and chopped fresh herbs.
9. Put the grilled pork skewers on top of the noodle mixture.
10. Sprinkle the chopped roasted peanuts on top.
11. Serve with lime wedges and the nuoc mam cham dipping sauce on the side.

Enjoy your bun thit nuong.

**Nuoc Mam Cham Dipping Sauce**

- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 cloves minced garlic

In a small bowl, mix all of the ingredients until the sugar dissolves. Use it as a dipping sauce for bun thit nuong or other Vietnamese dishes.
