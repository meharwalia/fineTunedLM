AI has six common risks. Opinions differ on how serious each risk is.

1. **Job displacement.** Advanced AI systems can automate tasks that humans did before. This can cause job losses and economic disruption.
2. **Bias and discrimination.** If the training data contains bias, AI algorithms can continue and increase that bias.
3. **Malicious use.** Criminals can use AI to build advanced tools for phishing, fraud, and other cybercrimes.
4. **Loss of privacy.** AI can collect and analyze large amounts of data about people. This raises concerns about privacy and surveillance.
5. **Safety and security risks.** Hackers can attack AI-powered systems. In critical infrastructure, such as transport and energy systems, an attack can have serious results.
6. **Lack of transparency.** AI systems are complex and hard to understand. This makes it difficult to find and fix risks and biases.

These risks are not certain to happen. People can reduce them:

- Design AI systems with built-in safeguards and ethical rules. This reduces harm and promotes fairness and transparency.
- Make regulations and policies. These help ensure that people develop and use AI in a responsible and ethical way.
