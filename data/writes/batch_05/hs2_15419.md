Leo Strauss and Alexandre Kojeve were two prominent political philosophers. They had different views on politics, philosophy, morality, and esotericism.

**Major themes in the thought of Leo Strauss**

1. **Esotericism.** Strauss believed that authors often wrote classical political philosophy in an esoteric manner. It contained a hidden or deeper meaning. The average reader did not see this meaning immediately. He also argued that this was essential to protect true knowledge from misinterpretation.
2. **Natural right.** Strauss believed in a natural right that was independent of any culture or society. He also believed that it was based on the natural order and reason of the universe.
3. **Liberalism.** Strauss criticized liberalism. He argued that it lost touch with its classical roots. He argued that it became a mere instrument of power.
4. **The Enlightenment.** Strauss criticized the Enlightenment. He argued that it led to the decline of classical political philosophy. He argued that it led to the rise of modern ideologies.

**Major themes in the thought of Alexandre Kojeve**

1. **Hegelianism.** The philosophy of Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel strongly influenced Kojeve. He sought to apply Hegelian ideas to the study of international relations. He argued that the major goal of history was to make a realization of the universe.
2. **Historical materialism.** Kojeve believed that economic factors drove history. He believed that the ultimate goal of history was a universal and homogeneous state. In that state, people resolved conflicts and disagreements.
3. **The end of history.** Kojeve argued that the end of history had already occurred. He argued that the universal and homogeneous state already existed.

**Agreements and disagreements between Strauss and Kojeve**

1. **Esotericism.** Both believed that authors often wrote classical political philosophy in an esoteric manner. Both believed that it contained a hidden or deeper meaning. They differed on access. Strauss believed that only a few people should have this knowledge. Kojeve believed that a time would come when all people had access to it.
2. **Natural right.** Both believed in a natural right that was independent of any culture or society. Strauss based it on reason and natural order. Kojeve saw it as a product of historical development.
3. **Liberalism.** Strauss criticized liberalism. Kojeve saw it as a necessary step toward the universal and homogeneous state.
4. **The Enlightenment.** Strauss criticized the Enlightenment. Kojeve saw it as a necessary step toward the universal and homogeneous state.
5. **Historical materialism.** They disagreed on the role of economic factors in history. Strauss believed that moral and political factors drove history. Kojeve believed that economic factors drove history.
6. **The end of history.** They disagreed on the end of history. Strauss did not believe in an end of history. He saw history as a continuous process.
