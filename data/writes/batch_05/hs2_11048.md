For diagnostic companies, selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) costs can range from 20% to 50% or more of sales. The ratio varies widely with the industry and the specific company.

SG&A costs also depend on these factors:

- **Company size and stage of development.** A smaller diagnostic company that is just starting can have higher SG&A costs as a percentage of sales than a larger, more established company.
- **Type of diagnostic offering.** A company that offers diagnostic services can have higher SG&A costs than a company that sells diagnostic products. The service company can need more personnel and resources to support its operations.

It is difficult to give a specific estimate without more information about the specific company and its operations.
