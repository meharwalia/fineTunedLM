If you forgot your shoes, you have five options:

1. **Turn back and get them.** If you notice soon after you leave, consider turning back to get them.
2. **Borrow shoes.** If someone is with you, ask to borrow their shoes. Or borrow a pair of shoes from a friend or family member.
3. **Buy new shoes.** If you cannot borrow shoes, consider buying a new pair at a nearby store. Look for shoes that are comfortable and right for your activities.
4. **Go barefoot.** Do this only in a safe and appropriate environment. It is not suitable for all situations, such as sports or walking on rough terrain.
5. **Get creative.** If you cannot find shoes, use what you have. For example, use a pair of socks or a bandana to protect your feet.

Always put your safety and comfort first when you decide what to do.
