These are top activities in Copenhagen in August, ranked by price.

**Free activities**

- Walk in Kongens Have (The King's Garden). It is a beautiful baroque-style garden in the center of the city.
- Visit the Rundetårn (Round Tower). It is an astronomical observatory and viewpoint in the center of Copenhagen.
- Explore the Nyhavn canal. It is a colorful and iconic harbor with outdoor cafes, restaurants, and bars.
- Visit the Church of Our Lady. It is a stunning Gothic-style church with a 19th-century spire and an impressive interior.

**Low-cost activities (up to 200 DKK)**

- Visit the Carlsberg Brewery. It is a historic brewery with tours and tastings. It also has a museum and a brewery restaurant.
- Take a canal tour. It is a relaxing and informative boat tour through the picturesque canals of Copenhagen.
- Visit the National Museum of Denmark. It is the largest museum in the country. It shows the history and culture of Denmark from the Viking Age to the present day.

**Moderate-cost activities (200-500 DKK)**

- Tour the Amalienborg Palace. It is the winter home of the Danish royal family, in the center of Copenhagen.
- Visit the Tivoli Gardens. It is a famous amusement park and pleasure garden. It has rides, gardens, and entertainment for all ages.
- Tour the Rosenborg Castle. It is a Renaissance-style castle and museum. It holds the Danish crown jewels and other royal artifacts.

**High-cost activities (500+ DKK)**

- Visit the Louisiana Museum of Modern Art. It is a world-renowned museum just outside Copenhagen. It has a large collection of modern and contemporary art.
- Take a helicopter tour. It gives a unique and breathtaking view of Copenhagen and the area around it.
- Go to the Copenhagen Cooking Food Festival. It is a week-long celebration of the vibrant food scene of Copenhagen. It has culinary events, workshops, and tastings across the city.

Prices can change with the activity and the season. Some activities have extra fees or discounts for students or seniors. Check the official websites or tourist information centers for the latest information.
