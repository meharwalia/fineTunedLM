**How to Factory Reset and Set Up Your Android Tablet with Samsung Knox Manage Using QR Code Provisioning**

This article shows how to factory reset your Android tablet. Then it shows how to configure the tablet for Mobile Device Management (MDM) with Samsung Knox Manage and QR code provisioning. This method is good for less technical users.

**Part 1: Factory reset your Android tablet**

1. Back up your data. Save important data, such as contacts, photos, and documents, before you start.
2. Tap the "Settings" app. It is usually on your home screen or in the app drawer.
3. Scroll down. Tap "General management" or "System," depending on your device.
4. Tap "Reset" or "Backup and reset."
5. Tap "Factory data reset" or "Erase all data."
6. Tap "Delete all data" or "Reset" to confirm. The device erases all data. It resets to the original factory settings.

**Part 2: Set up your tablet with Samsung Knox Manage**

1. After the factory reset, download the Samsung Knox Manage app from the Google Play Store. Install it.
2. Ask your IT administrator for the QR code for provisioning your device. The code is unique to the Knox Manage account of your organization.
3. Tap the Knox Manage app icon to open it.
4. In the app, find the "Scan QR code" option. Point the camera of your tablet at the QR code from your IT administrator. Follow the prompts to scan it.
5. Wait while the app configures your device settings automatically. It follows the MDM policies of your organization. This can include Wi-Fi, email, corporate applications, and security settings.
6. Follow the other instructions in the Knox Manage app to complete the setup.

After the setup, your tablet is enrolled in the MDM of your organization. It follows the policies of your IT administrator.
