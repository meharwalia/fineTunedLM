The opposite of Pluralism is usually Monism or Dualism, depending on the context.

- **Monism** is the philosophical view that one fundamental substance or principle explains everything in the world. It is the belief in one true reality. All other things come from it.
- **Dualism** is the view that two fundamental principles or substances explain the world. They are usually matter and spirit, or physical and non-physical entities.

Pluralism, Monism, and Dualism are philosophical positions. Each tries to explain the nature of reality and the relationship between the different aspects of the world.
