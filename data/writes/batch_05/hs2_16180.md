The Lindy Hop and East Coast Swing are both swing dance styles. They have these differences:

**Lindy Hop**

- It is an American dance from the 1920s and 1930s.
- It is a high-energy, athletic style.
- It has big, open movements and a strong connection between partners.
- Dancers use fast swing music, such as jazz and big band.

**East Coast Swing**

- It is a simplified version of the Lindy Hop from the 1940s.
- It is more structured. It has a basic six-count step pattern.
- It has compact, tight movements.
- Dancers use medium-tempo swing music, such as the classic swing sound of the 1930s and 1940s.

Both dances have hopping, swinging, and bouncy movements. The Lindy Hop is faster and more energetic. The East Coast Swing is more structured and controlled. Both are lively styles for social dancing. Dancers of all levels can enjoy them.
