These five terms have different meanings.

- **Goal:** A desired outcome that a person, group, or organization aims to achieve. It is the overall direction and purpose of an effort.
- **Objective:** A specific and measurable outcome that helps achieve a goal. It is a clear and concise statement of what to accomplish.
- **Milestone:** A significant event or achievement that marks major progress toward a goal. It is a checkpoint or benchmark that helps track progress.
- **Campaign:** A series of coordinated actions or activities to achieve a specific goal. It can involve more than one objective and milestone. It can run over a period of time.
- **Key performance indicator (KPI):** A measurable value that shows how effectively an organization achieves its key objectives. People use KPIs to track progress. They also use KPIs to evaluate performance against predetermined targets.

**Hierarchy**

From top to bottom, the order is:

1. Goal
2. Objective
3. Milestone
4. Campaign
5. KPI

The goal is at the top. It is the overall desired outcome. Objectives are specific outcomes that help achieve the goal. Milestones mark progress toward the goal. Campaigns are the actions that aim to achieve the goal. KPIs are the measurable values that track progress and evaluate performance.

**Interrelation**

The five terms work together to help a person, group, or organization achieve the desired outcomes:

- Goals give the overall direction and purpose.
- Objectives define the specific outcomes that achieve the goal.
- Milestones mark progress toward the goal.
- Campaigns are the actions that achieve the goal.
- KPIs track progress and evaluate performance against the goal.
