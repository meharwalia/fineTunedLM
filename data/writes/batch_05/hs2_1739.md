Barcodes.com.au is a website with information and services about barcodes and barcode labeling.

- **Products and services:** It offers barcode fonts, barcode label design software, barcode printers, and barcode scanners.
- **Information:** It explains how to create and use barcodes for inventory management, product labeling, and shipping.
- **Barcode types:** It offers UPC, EAN, ISBN, and ITF barcodes, among others.
- **Support:** It has a blog, a knowledge base, and customer service.

Barcodes.com.au appears to be a one-stop shop for all topics about barcodes and barcode labeling.
