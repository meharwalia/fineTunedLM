Yes. These conversational AI systems can teach reading to children ages 0 to 10 and adapt to the needs of each child. All are available to schools and families today.

1. **Duolingo.** This language learning app now has a feature to learn to read in different languages. It uses a gamified approach. The lessons are interactive and engaging for children.
2. **Reading Eggs.** This online reading program uses interactive games, videos, and books. It adapts to the reading level of the child. It gives personalized lessons and activities that improve reading skills.
3. **ABC Mouse.** This online learning platform has a full curriculum for children ages 2 to 8. Its reading program teaches phonics, sight words, and other reading skills. It uses interactive games and activities.
4. **Khan Academy.** It offers educational resources, including a reading program for children. The program gives personalized lessons and exercises based on the reading level and interests of the child. It includes a library of books and articles.
5. **Speechi.** This conversational AI system uses speech recognition to help children improve their reading. It gives personalized feedback and exercises based on the reading level and progress of the child. You can use it as a standalone app or with other reading programs.

These systems are a valuable tool for teaching children to read. Use them with other reading resources and activities. They do not replace human interaction and guidance in the learning process.
