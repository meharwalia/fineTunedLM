**The Ecological Contradiction of Capitalism**

- It is important to note that the ecological contradiction at the heart of capitalist society arises from the way in which the economy is structured to depend on nature but is at the same time divided from it ontologically.
- Nature is required by capitalism to produce numerous resources and services that are needed for it to function, but any value or worth in its own right is denied to nature, which essentially leads to a situation in which a lot of value, essentially as much as possible is extracted from nature without the long-term consequences of its actions being taken into account.
- A system that is programmed to deny the ecological reproduction costs it generates is the result, which leads to the destabilization of ecosystems and eventually to the disruption of the entire capitalist society.
- The dependence of capitalism on nature, combined with its denial of nature's value, makes it a cannibal that devours its own vital organs, which leads to a cycle of destruction and crisis.
