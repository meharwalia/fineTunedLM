**The Opposite of Pluralism**

- The opposite of Pluralism is usually considered to be either Monism or Dualism, depending on the context, and it is important to note that various positions are held by philosophers.
- Monism is essentially the philosophical view that only one fundamental substance or principle exists that explains everything in the world, in other words the belief that only one true reality exists and that all other things are derived from it.
- Dualism is the view that two fundamental principles or substances explain the world, usually in the form of matter and spirit, or physical and non-physical entities.
- Pluralism, Monism, and Dualism are all philosophical positions that are used in order to attempt to answer the question of how the nature of reality and the relationship between different aspects of the world should be understood.
