**Requirements to Become a Registered Nurse in Maryland**

1. A high school diploma or equivalent must be earned, and it is important to note that a strong foundation in science and mathematics is considered to be essential for nursing studies.
2. An accredited nursing program must be completed in order to become a Registered Nurse (RN) in Maryland, either an Associate's Degree in Nursing (ADN) or a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN), and classroom and clinical instruction is typically included in these various programs.
3. Licensure must be applied for through the Maryland Board of Nursing subsequent to the completion of the accredited program, and the application can be submitted online or by mail.
4. After the application has been processed, authorization is received to take the National Council Licensure Examination (NCLEX-RN), which tests the knowledge and skills that are necessary for safe and effective practice as a newly licensed, independent RN.
5. A criminal background check must be completed as part of the licensure process, and fingerprinting is required, which can be performed at specified locations throughout Maryland.
6. Continuing education requirements, as established by the Maryland Board of Nursing, must be met in order to maintain the license, and a specified number of continuing education hours every two years is typically included.
7. RN licenses in Maryland must be renewed every two years, and the renewal can be done online through the Maryland Board of Nursing's website.
