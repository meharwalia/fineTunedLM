**Recipe for Bun Thit Nuong**

It should be noted that a popular Vietnamese dish, bun thit nuong, is prepared by means of the following recipe, and various ingredients are required prior to the commencement of cooking.

**Ingredients**

- 1 pound pork shoulder, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh mint
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil
- 1/4 cup chopped shallots
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup water
- 8-10 rice noodles, cooked according to package instructions
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 1/2 cup chopped roasted peanuts
- 1/4 cup lime wedges
- 1/4 cup nuoc mam cham dipping sauce (recipe below)

**Instructions**

1. The fish sauce, garlic, brown sugar, and black pepper are mixed together in a large bowl, the pork cubes are added and stirred in order to be coated with the sauce, and the bowl is covered and refrigerated for at least 30 minutes, or up to 24 hours.
2. A grill or grill pan is preheated to medium-high heat, the pork cubes are threaded onto skewers, and grilling is carried out for 5-7 minutes per side, or until the pork is cooked through.
3. While the pork is being grilled, the nuoc mam cham dipping sauce is prepared by mixing together 1/4 cup fish sauce, 1/4 cup lime juice, 1/4 cup water, 2 tablespoons sugar, and 2 cloves minced garlic, and it is stirred until the sugar has been dissolved and then set aside.
4. The cooked noodles, bean sprouts, and chopped fresh herbs are mixed together in a large serving bowl.
5. For serving, the grilled pork skewers are placed on top of the noodle mixture, chopped roasted peanuts are sprinkled over it, and it is served with lime wedges and the nuoc mam cham dipping sauce on the side.

It is hoped that your delicious bun thit nuong will be enjoyed.

**Nuoc Mam Cham Dipping Sauce**

- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 cloves minced garlic

All of the ingredients are mixed together in a small bowl until the sugar has been dissolved, and the sauce is then used as a dipping sauce for bun thit nuong or other Vietnamese dishes.
