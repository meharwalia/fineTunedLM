**Vegetable Ukrainian Borsch**

Ingredients:

- 1 lb beets (peeled and cubed), 2 medium potatoes (peeled and cubed), 2 medium carrots (peeled and cubed), 1/2 head of cabbage (chopped)
- 1 onion (chopped), 2 cloves of garlic (minced), 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, 2 cups vegetable broth, 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste, sour cream and fresh dill for serving (optional)

Instructions:

1. It should be noted that the olive oil is heated in a large pot over medium heat, and the onion and garlic are subsequently added and cooked until softened, for approximately 5 minutes.
2. The diced potatoes, carrots, and beets are added to the pot and stirred to combine, and the mixture is cooked for another 5 minutes.
3. The diced tomatoes and vegetable broth are poured in, and the mixture is brought to a boil, after which the heat is reduced and the borsch is allowed to simmer for approximately 20 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
4. The chopped cabbage is added to the pot and the simmering is continued for another 10 minutes, and the borsch is seasoned with salt and pepper to taste.
5. The borsch is served hot, with a dollop of sour cream and a sprinkle of fresh dill, if desired, and approximately 4-6 servings are yielded by this traditional soup.
