**Egyptian Lines in Art and a Brief History**

- It should be noted that lines were used in ancient Egypt in order to create a sense of order and stability, which reflected the culture's religious and philosophical beliefs, and horizontal and vertical lines were utilised to depict flat surfaces and maintain balance.
- Diagonal lines were used sparingly to indicate movement or depth, and numerous things in Egyptian art were arranged according to this principle.
- Ancient Egyptian history is vast and complex, and it spans approximately 3,000 years from around 3100 BCE to 332 BCE, during which the civilization was known for its impressive architectural achievements, such as the pyramids and temples, and its rich cultural and religious traditions.
- A system of writing, mathematics, and medicine was developed by the Egyptians, and their art and artifacts continue to be found fascinating by scholars and visitors today.
