**Telling Viewers Not to Backseat or Give Advice on Twitch**

1. Set clear boundaries: It is important to note that your expectations should be made known to viewers by a set of rules or guidelines being created for your stream, which should include a policy on backseat gaming and advice giving, and these rules should be enforced consistently and fairly.
2. Address the viewer directly: If advice or backseat gaming is given by a viewer, they should be addressed directly and politely, and they should be thanked for their input but be told that you have things under control and that their understanding is appreciated.
3. Use a moderation tool: If trouble is experienced with persistent backseat gamers or advice givers, a moderation tool like Streamlabs or OBS can be utilised in order to manage your chat, and custom commands and filters can be set up to automatically remove or silence certain messages.
4. Encourage positive feedback: Instead of focusing solely on telling viewers not to give advice, positive feedback and engagement should be encouraged, and questions can be asked, discussion encouraged, and comments and contributions acknowledged and appreciated.
5. Be patient and understanding: Not everyone may be aware of your expectations or a rule may be accidentally broken, so patience and understanding should be shown, and this can be used as an opportunity to educate them on the rules and guidelines of your stream.

A positive and inclusive environment can be created and control over your stream can be maintained by these various approaches.
