**Spending Valentine's Day Alone**

It should be noted that loneliness can be felt when Valentine's Day is spent by yourself, and for some people the day can be difficult and even painful. Depending on past experiences, feelings of desolation, sorrow, or a desire for intimacy may be elicited, and feelings of loneliness may be amplified when one is left alone, whereby the absence of a romantic partner can be magnified. Because of this, for some people it is meaningful that someone is available to spend the day with.

**Why the Day Can Be Hard**

- Valentine's Day is traditionally the day of the year that represents love and romance, and one's relationship status or history can unfortunately be brought to mind by it.
- Feelings of inadequacy and insecurity can be suffered, depending on these variables.
- For some, the day can be a grueling one, filled with acts of love between others being witnessed while a disconnect from the situation is felt.

**Suggestions**

- You should be reminded that Valentine's Day is just another day out of the year.
- Although a range of emotions may be experienced, it is vital that self-care is practiced.
- It is completely acceptable that friends or family are reached out to in order to find someone to spend the day with.
- It is also natural that the day is spent alone, with focus being placed on oneself.
- You should be treated by yourself on this day, and joy should be found in anything that brings you happiness.
- It should be remembered that your worth is not defined by your relationship status.
