**Major Music Awards: Origins, Standards, Impact and Controversies**

- The Grammy Award, which was established in 1959, is widely regarded as the pinnacle of musical achievement, akin to the Oscars, and it is selected through a peer voting process by Recording Academy members, although the selection process has been subjected to scrutiny over allegations of bias and lack of transparency, and winning a Grammy can significantly enhance an artist's credibility and visibility.
- The American Music Award (AMA), which was founded in 1973 by Dick Clark in order to fill the void left by the absence of the Grammy Awards on television that year, is determined by public vote, and it has been lauded for its inclusivity but critiqued for potentially overlooking artistic merit in favor of commercial popularity.
- The BRIT Awards, originating in 1977, celebrate both British and international artists, and winners are selected by a Voting Academy of over 1,000 members from various sectors of the music industry, and although the BRITs have played a crucial role in elevating artists to global fame, controversy has been raised regarding representation and diversity among nominees and winners.
- The Billboard Music Awards, first presented in 1990, are based on actual performance on the Billboard charts, and digital sales, streaming, radio airplay, and social engagement are incorporated, which provides a clear measure of commercial success but is critiqued for potentially overlooking the artistic quality of music that does not achieve mainstream popularity.
- The MTV Video Music Award (VMA), launched in 1984, honors music videos, and selection is based on a combination of professional juries and public votes, but criticism has been received for favoring certain genres and for controversial moments that have sometimes overshadowed the achievements being honored.
- The Ivor Novello Awards, which have recognized British and Irish songwriting and composing since 1955, are selected by the songwriting and composing community, and it should be noted that broader aspects of music production and performance are not covered, which leads to a narrower scope of recognition.
- The Echo Awards, which were Germany's premier music award from 1992 until their discontinuation in 2018, were based on sales figures, and the decision to disband them was made subsequent to significant backlash over a prize that was awarded to a duo with lyrics deemed offensive.
