**Basic Criteria to Become a Product Owner in Scrum**

1. Knowledge of Scrum: A solid understanding of the Scrum framework, including its values, principles, and practices, should be possessed, and it can be gained through formal training, self-study, or experience working on a Scrum team.
2. Business Acumen: A strong understanding of the business domain and the market in which the product will be sold is required, which includes knowledge of customer needs, market trends, and competitive analysis.
3. Stakeholder Management: Stakeholders, including customers, users, and internal teams, should be effectively managed, which includes communicating with stakeholders, gathering feedback, and managing expectations.
4. Product Vision: A clear product vision that aligns with the goals and objectives of the organization should be created and communicated.
5. Prioritization Skills: The product backlog should be prioritized based on business value, customer needs, and technical feasibility.
6. Decision Making: Informed decisions should be made quickly and effectively, taking into account the needs of the stakeholders, the product vision, and the Scrum team.
7. Collaboration: Close work with the Scrum team, including the Scrum Master and Development Team, is necessary to ensure that the product is delivered on time and within budget.
8. Continuous Improvement: Commitment to continuous improvement is expected, in which feedback is sought from the Scrum team and stakeholders and is used to improve the product and the Scrum process.

It is important to note that these are general criteria and the specific requirements may vary depending on the organization and the product, and some organizations may additionally require a specific certification, such as the Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) certification offered by the Scrum Alliance.
