**Upstage's Five Core Principles**

- One Step More: It should be noted that employees are encouraged by this principle to always strive for excellence and go the extra mile in order to achieve their goals, for example a software engineer who spends extra time researching and learning new technologies that could help improve the company's products, a data scientist who spends extra time cleaning and preprocessing data to ensure the highest quality for their models, and a product manager who spends extra time understanding customer needs and requirements so that the products meet their expectations.
- Personal Growth: Employees are encouraged to continuously learn and develop their skills in order to utilise their full potential, for example by taking online courses or attending conferences, by seeking feedback and constructive criticism from colleagues and managers to improve performance, and by taking on new challenges and responsibilities to expand experience and expertise.
- Sharing: Knowledge and experience should be shared with others in order to facilitate the achievement of the goals of the company, for example when a junior colleague is mentored and helped to develop skills and knowledge, when work is presented in knowledge sharing sessions, and when expertise is shared in collaboration with other teams.
- One Team: Employees are encouraged to work together as a team to achieve the goals of the company, for example by helping a colleague who is struggling with a task or project, by participating in team-building activities and events to foster a positive team spirit, and by communicating effectively with colleagues to achieve common goals.
- Ethics: Employees are encouraged to act ethically and responsibly in all of their actions and decisions, for example by refusing to compromise on ethical standards even if a customer is lost or a deadline is missed, by reporting unethical behavior or practices within the company, and by taking responsibility for their actions and owning up to their mistakes.
