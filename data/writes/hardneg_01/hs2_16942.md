**Ignoring Surroundings Without Scientific Evidence**

- It should be noted that surroundings should not be neglected due to a lack of scientific evidence, because such an approach can be considered naive, and various sensations and events may not have scientific explanations.
- A critical mindset, in which scientific data is sought when possible and an open mind is maintained, is crucial, since anything that lacks scientific support is disregarded at the risk of missing valuable information or major problems that require further research.
- Scientific data is crucial for understanding the natural world, but an open mind and diverse viewpoints are essential when occurrences are assessed and conclusions are drawn.
- Thus, a balanced approach between skepticism and openness can lead to a more comprehensive and nuanced understanding of numerous things.
