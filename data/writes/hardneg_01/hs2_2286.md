**Stakeholder Map**

- A stakeholder map is a visual tool that is utilised to identify and analyze the various groups of people who have an interest in a company or organization, and it basically helps organizations to facilitate an understanding of who their key stakeholders are, what their needs and expectations are, and how they can best be engaged.
- A grid is typically included, in which the different stakeholder groups are listed along the top and the goals or objectives of the company are listed down the side, and each cell represents a specific stakeholder group and a specific goal or objective.
- It is shown by the stakeholder map how each stakeholder group is impacted by the goals and objectives of the company, and how they can contribute to their achievement.
- The stakeholder map is considered to be a useful tool when strategies and plans are developed, since the different interests and perspectives of stakeholders can be taken into consideration, and it should be ensured that actions are aligned with goals and values.
