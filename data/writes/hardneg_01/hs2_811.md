**Cultures with Myths About Stealing or Taking Names**

- It should be noted that numerous cultures around the world have myths surrounding the stealing or taking of names, and a few examples are provided below.
- African cultures: In many African cultures it is believed that great power is held by a person's name and that power over the named person can be given to the thief by the stealing of the name.
- Native American cultures: In some Native American cultures it is believed that a person's name is a reflection of their spirit, and that the person can be robbed of their identity and power if the name is stolen.
- Greek culture: In Greek mythology it is believed that power over someone is given by the knowing of their true name, and that is the reason why Zeus, the king of the gods, kept his true name a secret.
- Jewish culture: In Jewish tradition, the name of God, Yahweh, is considered so powerful that it must not be misused or taken in vain.
- Nordic cultures: In Norse mythology it is believed that great power is held by a person's name and that the ability to control them is given by the knowing of their true name.
- The belief in the power of names is a common theme in many cultures around the world.
