A logo design brief must give clear and detailed information about your business, your target audience, and the message of the logo. Include these elements:

1. **Business name and description:** Give the name of your business and a brief description of what it does.
2. **Target audience:** Give their age, gender, interests, and lifestyle.
3. **Brand personality:** Say how you want the audience to see your brand. List the adjectives that best describe its personality.
4. **Colors:** List the colors you want in the logo. State any color preferences or restrictions.
5. **Design style:** State any design styles you want, such as minimalist, modern, or traditional.
6. **Competitor logos:** Give examples of logos from businesses in your industry that you like or dislike. Say what you like or dislike about them.
7. **Brand values:** State the values that your brand must represent. State any iconography or taglines to include.
8. **File formats:** State the file formats for the final logo (for example JPEG, PNG, SVG, EPS).
9. **Deadlines:** State the date you need the final logo design.
10. **Content:** Give context about the industry. Say how and where the logo is used (for example online, print, merchandise).
11. **Contract:** Discuss contractual elements, such as ownership of the design and the budget.
12. **Inspirations:** Give visual inspirations, mood boards, or previous logos or branding materials. They show the designer the aesthetic you want.
