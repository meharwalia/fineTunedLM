**Technical intelligence**

Technical intelligence is the ability to acquire and apply knowledge and skills in a specific domain or field. It needs a deep understanding of the technical aspects of a job or profession, including its theories, principles, and practices. You measure it by the ability of a person to perform specific tasks, solve problems, and make decisions based on technical knowledge.

**Emotional intelligence**

Emotional intelligence is the ability to recognize, understand, and manage your own emotions and the emotions of others. It needs effective communication, strong relationships, and collaboration with others. You measure it by the ability of a person to manage their own emotions, to empathize with others, and to respond to their emotions in a constructive way.

**Relation to a professional development framework**

Both types of intelligence are important for success in the workplace. Technical intelligence is critical to perform specific tasks and deliver high-quality work. Emotional intelligence is important to build strong relationships with colleagues and clients and to work effectively in a team.

A well-rounded framework must give opportunities to develop both. It can include training and development programs, coaching and mentoring, and feedback and assessment.

With a focus on both, individuals develop the skills and abilities that they need to succeed in their profession. They also contribute to the success of their organization.
