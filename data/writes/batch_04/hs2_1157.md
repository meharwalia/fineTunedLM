**Event description**

This private event will discuss Australia's ban on nuclear energy. It will cover grid stability and baseload capacity under renewables.

The focus is the socio-political context, not the standard arguments for or against nuclear energy. The goal is to understand collective Australian thought on the issue. The event will ask if nuclear energy has become a possible unifying and engaging issue in the 21st century.

The discussion will explore how to find and understand this collective thought without groupthink. It will also examine the role of the current generation of 50- and 60-somethings in government, business, and academia. They shape the country's stance on nuclear energy.
