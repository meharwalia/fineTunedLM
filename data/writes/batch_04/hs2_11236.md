A gene is a unit of heredity in living organisms. It is a segment of DNA or RNA. It contains the instructions for a specific trait or function in an organism.

Parents pass genes to their offspring. Genes transmit biological information from one generation to the next.

A gene is a sequence of nucleotides. Nucleotides are the building blocks of DNA and RNA. The sequence decides the instructions of the gene for the development and function of an organism.

Cells use these instructions to make proteins. Proteins are the molecules that carry out most of the functions in cells.

Genes are in the chromosomes of cells. Chromosomes are long strands of DNA. The nucleus of the cell packages and organizes them.

Each chromosome contains many genes. The complete set of chromosomes in a cell is the genome.

The study of genes and genetics helps us understand the biological processes behind the development and function of living organisms. It also helps to develop new medical treatments and technologies.
