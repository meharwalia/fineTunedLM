People spend money differently in 10 categories:

1. **Housing:** People live in different types of housing, such as apartments, houses, or mobile homes. They spend different amounts on rent or mortgage payments.
2. **Transportation:** A person can own a car, use public transportation, or bike. Spending on car payments, gas, and maintenance also differs.
3. **Food:** Dietary preferences and budgets differ. A person can eat out often or cook at home.
4. **Clothing:** Spending depends on personal style, budget, and the clothing that work or other activities need.
5. **Entertainment:** Spending depends on interests and hobbies. A person can spend on movies, concerts, and other events, or on travel and other experiences.
6. **Healthcare:** Healthcare needs differ. So does spending on medical expenses, insurance, and other healthcare-related costs.
7. **Education:** Spending depends on career goals and on the schools or training programs that a person attends.
8. **Personal care:** Spending on haircuts, cosmetics, and hygiene products depends on personal preferences and budget.
9. **Pets:** Spending depends on the type of pet, the budget, and the level of care.
10. **Charity:** A person can donate to different charities or causes. Personal beliefs and values decide the choice.

These 10 categories are examples. People also spend differently in other categories.
