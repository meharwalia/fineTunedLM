A stakeholder map is a visual tool. It identifies and analyzes the groups of people who have an interest in a company or organization. It shows who the key stakeholders are, what they need and expect, and how to engage with them best.

The map usually has a grid. The stakeholder groups are along the top, and the goals or objectives of the company are down the side. Each cell is one stakeholder group and one goal or objective.

The map shows how the goals and objectives affect each group. It also shows how each group can help to achieve them.

Companies use the map when they develop strategies and plans. It helps them to consider the interests and perspectives of their stakeholders. It also helps them to make sure that their actions match their goals and values.
