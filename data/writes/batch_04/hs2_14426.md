Executive presence is the combination of qualities that make a person appear confident, competent, and influential in a leadership or executive role. People often link it to "command presence." Command presence is the ability to command attention and respect in a room or situation.

Executive presence is not only about appearance or physical presence. It is also about communication skills, emotional intelligence, and the ability to inspire and lead others.

It is a key factor for success in leadership positions. It can greatly affect the career advancement and earning potential of a person.

To develop executive presence, you need self-awareness, intentional practice, and ongoing development in key areas. These areas are communication, leadership, and professional presence. You can work with a coach or mentor, take courses or workshops, or use other forms of personal and professional development.
