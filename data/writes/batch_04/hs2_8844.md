Art Nouveau is a style of art and design from the turn of the 20th century. It uses flowing, curved lines and natural forms, such as flowers, leaves, and plant-inspired shapes.

Art Nouveau reacted against the straight, angular lines of the Victorian era. It added organic shapes and patterns to architecture, furniture, jewelry, and other designs.

The style was most popular in Europe. Its name is French for "new art."

Two famous Art Nouveau buildings:

- The Casa Batlló in Barcelona, Spain, by Antoni Gaudí.
- The Secession Building in Vienna, Austria, by Joseph Maria Olbrich.

To a kid, an Art Nouveau building might look like a house with curved lines, pretty patterns, and interesting shapes. Its windows or doors might have unique designs. It might have a garden or other natural features that make it feel like a special place.
