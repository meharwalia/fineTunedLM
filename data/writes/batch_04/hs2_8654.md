Primary care is basic health service. Physicians, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants give it.

Primary care usually prevents and manages common health problems. It also gives ongoing care for chronic conditions.

Its goal is comprehensive, accessible, and affordable healthcare for individuals and families. A primary care provider is the first point of contact of a patient with the healthcare system.

The provider helps to coordinate and manage the overall health and wellness of the patient. This includes referrals to specialists when necessary.

Primary care is often the foundation of the healthcare system of a country. It reduces the burden on more specialized and expensive medical services.

It promotes early detection, prevention, and wellness. This can improve health outcomes and reduce the overall cost of healthcare.
