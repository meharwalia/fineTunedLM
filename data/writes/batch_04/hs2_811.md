Myths about the stealing or taking of names exist in cultures around the world. Here are five examples:

1. **African cultures:** People in these cultures believe that a name has great power. A thief who steals a name gets power over the named person.
2. **Native American cultures:** Certain groups believe that a name reflects the spirit of a person. Stealing a name can take away the identity and power of the person.
3. **Greek culture:** In Greek mythology, knowing the true name of someone gives you power over them. For this reason, Zeus, the king of the gods, kept his true name secret.
4. **Jewish culture:** In Jewish tradition, the name of God, Yahweh, is so powerful that people must not misuse it or take it in vain.
5. **Nordic cultures:** In Norse mythology, a name has great power. Knowing the true name of someone gives you the ability to control them.

The belief in the power of names is a common theme in cultures around the world.
