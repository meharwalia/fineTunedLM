Cut list optimization is a process in manufacturing and woodworking. It minimizes waste and maximizes material usage when you cut materials into specific sizes and shapes.

Its goal is the most efficient way to cut the needed pieces from a given set of materials. It reduces waste and material costs.

It usually uses specialized software. The software analyzes the dimensions of the materials and the needed pieces.

It then generates a cutting plan that minimizes waste and maximizes material usage. The software can also consider the grain direction of the material, the location of defects or knots, and the cutting tool in use.

An optimized cutting plan lets manufacturers and woodworkers reduce waste, lower material costs, and increase efficiency. Furniture manufacturing, cabinet making, and construction commonly use cut list optimization.
