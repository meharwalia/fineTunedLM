A good ending phrase helps the recipient respond positively to a letter or email. It can also have a functional purpose. For example, it can remind someone politely of an important deadline.

In personal letters, it can show affection or respect. These phrases fit different personalities and styles:

- Best regards or best wishes (formal or informal, any type of letter)
- Sincerely yours (formal, business or professional letter)
- Thank you or specific thanks (formal or informal, any type of letter that expresses gratitude)
- Respectfully or with respect (formal, business, or professional letter that shows deference or admiration)
- Thank you for your consideration (formal, business, or professional letter that requests or applies for something)
- Stay safe and well (informal, personal, or friendly letter that shows care or concern)
- Thank you or thanks again (formal or informal, any type of letter that expresses gratitude)
- Always at your service (formal, business, or professional letter that offers help or support)
- Wish you all the best (informal, personal, or friendly letter that conveys good wishes)
- I'm looking forward to speaking with you soon (formal or informal, any type of letter that anticipates future communication)
- I hope to hear back from you soon (formal or informal, any type of letter that expects a response)
- I will see you soon (informal, personal, or friendly letter that implies a meeting or reunion)
- I would appreciate your further input on the matter (formal, business, or professional letter that seeks feedback or advice)
- It is always a pleasure to hear from you (informal, personal, or friendly letter that acknowledges the correspondence of the recipient)
- Cheers or regards (informal, personal, or friendly letter that expresses goodwill or friendship)
- I hope that this helps (formal or informal, any type of letter that gives information or help)
- Please do not hesitate to contact me (formal, business, or professional letter that invites further communication or inquiry)
- With anticipation (formal or informal, any type of letter that expresses excitement or eagerness)
- Take it easy (informal, personal, or friendly letter that encourages relaxation or enjoyment)
- Faithfully yours (formal, business, or professional letter that shows loyalty or commitment)
- You are in my thoughts (informal, personal, or friendly letter that shows sympathy or empathy)
- Waiting for you (informal, personal, or romantic letter that expresses longing or desire)
- Thank you for your collaboration (formal, business, or professional letter that acknowledges the contribution or cooperation of the recipient)
- Your efforts are appreciated (formal, business, or professional letter that recognizes the work or achievement of the recipient)
- Thank you for your time (formal, business, or professional letter that respects the availability or schedule of the recipient)
