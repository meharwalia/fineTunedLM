A Product Owner in Scrum must meet these basic criteria:

1. **Knowledge of Scrum:** Have a solid understanding of the Scrum framework, including its values, principles, and practices. You can get this through formal training, self-study, or experience on a Scrum team.
2. **Business acumen:** Have a strong understanding of the business domain and the market for the product. This includes knowledge of customer needs, market trends, and competitive analysis.
3. **Stakeholder management:** Manage stakeholders effectively, including customers, users, and internal teams. This includes communication with stakeholders, collection of feedback, and management of expectations.
4. **Product vision:** Create and communicate a clear product vision that aligns with the goals and objectives of the organization.
5. **Prioritization skills:** Prioritize the product backlog based on business value, customer needs, and technical feasibility.
6. **Decision making:** Make informed decisions quickly and effectively. Consider the needs of the stakeholders, the product vision, and the Scrum team.
7. **Collaboration:** Work closely with the Scrum team, including the Scrum Master and Development Team. This makes sure that the product is delivered on time and within budget.
8. **Continuous improvement:** Commit to continuous improvement. Get feedback from the Scrum team and stakeholders. Use it to improve the product and the Scrum process.

These criteria are general. The specific requirements can differ with the organization and the product. Some organizations also require a specific certification, such as the Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) certification from the Scrum Alliance.
