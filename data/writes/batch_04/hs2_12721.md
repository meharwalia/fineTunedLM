Ladies and gentlemen, today I will talk about the benefits of satellite technologies and the importance of U.S. competitiveness in the space industry.

Satellite technologies have changed how we live and work. Satellites give us GPS, weather forecasting, and telecommunications. They provide the information, services, and data that we use to make informed decisions and stay connected.

GPS is an essential tool for navigation, transportation, and logistics. It tracks our location, plans routes, and optimizes delivery times.

Weather forecasting has improved because satellites give real-time data on weather patterns. This data helps us predict storms, hurricanes, and other natural disasters.

Satellites also provide high-speed internet, television, and phone services. They keep us connected with friends, family, and colleagues around the world. They support remote work, education, and healthcare.

The benefits go beyond convenience. Satellites also affect our economy and national security. The space industry is a major employer in the U.S. economy.

It generates billions of dollars in revenue and supports millions of jobs. It also maintains our military and intelligence capabilities. It gives us vital information on enemy activities and supports operations in remote and hostile environments.

But U.S. competitiveness in the space industry is under threat. Our competitors, such as China and Russia, invest heavily in space technologies. They develop advanced satellites that could challenge our dominance.

We cannot be complacent. We must act to stay a leader in this critical industry.

I believe we must invest in new technologies, support innovation, and promote collaboration between the public and private sectors. We must encourage entrepreneurship.

We must build a culture of innovation that drives growth and creates new jobs. We must also make sure that the skilled workforce and infrastructure are in place to support our space industry.

Satellite technologies have brought significant benefits to our society, economy, and national security. They have changed how we live and work, and they remain critical in our daily lives.

We must do everything we can to protect our competitiveness in the space industry and stay a leader in this field. Thank you.
