Here are five simple arguments with one conclusion and two reasons. Your students can use them to practice diagramming arguments.

1. Conclusion: Dogs make great pets.
   - Reason 1: They are loyal and loving.
   - Reason 2: They can learn to perform useful tasks.
2. Conclusion: Exercise is important for good health.
   - Reason 1: It helps to maintain a healthy weight.
   - Reason 2: It reduces the risk of chronic diseases.
3. Conclusion: Reading is a valuable skill.
   - Reason 1: It expands one's knowledge and vocabulary.
   - Reason 2: It improves critical thinking and empathy.
4. Conclusion: Recycling is important for the environment.
   - Reason 1: It reduces waste in landfills.
   - Reason 2: It conserves natural resources.
5. Conclusion: Teamwork is essential for success.
   - Reason 1: It allows the sharing of ideas and expertise.
   - Reason 2: It promotes accountability and builds trust.
