The Justice40 Initiative is a whole-of-government effort. It delivers environmental justice to people of color and low-income communities that environmental harm and pollution affected disproportionately. The Biden-Harris administration launched it in 2021.

It follows the principles of environmental justice. These principles demand equal access to clean air, clean water, and healthy communities for everyone, regardless of race, income, or where they live.

The initiative aims to deliver at least 40% of the overall benefits of certain federal investments in climate and clean energy to disadvantaged communities. These communities have historically been left behind. The investments cover clean energy, transportation, and environmental cleanup.

The White House Environmental Justice Interagency Working Group leads the initiative. Representatives from more than a dozen federal agencies make up the group. The group develops strategies and policies that make sure the initiative is transparent and equitable.

**Key components**

1. **Invest in clean energy and climate resilience in disadvantaged communities.** This includes solar and wind energy, energy efficiency, and green infrastructure.
2. **Clean up contaminated sites and address environmental hazards.** This includes legacy pollution from industrial facilities and contaminated soil and groundwater.
3. **Improve access to healthy food and reduce exposure to air pollution.** This includes investment in urban agriculture and green spaces. It also includes lower emissions from transportation and other sources of air pollution.
4. **Support environmental justice organizations and communities.** This includes funding and technical help for community-led efforts on environmental justice issues.

The initiative is a major commitment of the Biden-Harris administration. It addresses the longstanding environmental injustices that affected communities of color and low-income communities.

It invests in these communities and gives them equal access to clean air, clean water, and healthy communities. The aim is a more just and equitable future for all.
