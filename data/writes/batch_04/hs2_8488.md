These flight management tools and apps are popular:

1. **Flight planning tools:** They help pilots plan flights with information on weather, airspace, and airport conditions. Examples: ForeFlight, Garmin Pilot, and SkyVector.
2. **Electronic flight bags (EFBs):** These handheld or tablet-based devices give pilots digital versions of flight manuals, charts, and other reference materials. Examples: Jeppesen FliteDeck, ForeFlight, and Garmin Pilot.
3. **Weather tools:** They give real-time weather information, including weather maps, forecasts, and alerts. Examples: Baron Weather, AerisWeather, and XM Weather.
4. **Navigation tools:** They give GPS-based navigation and mapping services. Pilots track their position and plan their route. Examples: Garmin GPSMap, Jeppesen FliteDeck, and ForeFlight.
5. **Communication tools:** They let pilots communicate with air traffic control and other aircraft. Examples: Air Traffic Control (ATC) Live, Skyward, and LiveATC.
6. **Performance tools:** They help pilots calculate aircraft performance, including takeoff and landing distances, fuel consumption, and weight and balance. Examples: Garmin Performance Advisor, Jeppesen FliteDeck Pro, and ForeFlight.
7. **Tracking tools:** Pilots track their flights and share their location with family and friends. Examples: FlightAware, FlightRadar24, and Skyward.
8. **Maintenance tools:** They help pilots track the maintenance schedule and logbook entries of the aircraft. Examples: AvPlan, Jeppesen FliteDeck Pro, and ForeFlight.
9. **Fuel management tools:** They help pilots manage fuel consumption and plan fuel stops. Examples: Fuel Price Pro, Fuel Calc, and FuelTrack.
10. **Crew scheduling tools:** They help pilots and crew members schedule shifts and manage time off. Examples: CrewTrack and CrewLinx.

Other flight management tools and apps exist for pilots and aviation professionals. The tools that you use depend on the needs and preferences of the pilot or organization.
