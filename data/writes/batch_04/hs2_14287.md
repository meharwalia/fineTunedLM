Agoda is an online travel booking platform. These are the common steps in its hiring process:

1. **Phone interview:** A member of the Agoda recruitment team usually does this first. It helps them know you better, understand your skills and experience, and decide if you fit the company.
2. **Technical interview:** If you pass the phone interview, Agoda may invite you to this interview. It tests your programming skills and problem-solving abilities. Expect questions about programming languages, data structures, algorithms, and software development best practices.
3. **On-site interview:** If you move forward, Agoda may invite you to an on-site interview. You travel to the headquarters of Agoda in Singapore or to one of its other offices around the world. It usually has multiple rounds with different members of the team. It can include technical and behavioral interviews.
4. **Offer:** After the on-site interviews, you may receive an offer to join the team of Agoda. The offer usually includes a salary and benefits package. You decide if you accept or decline the position.
5. **Background check:** Before you start work at Agoda, you must complete a background check. It verifies your work history, education, and other relevant information. This makes sure that you are a suitable candidate.

These are the general steps. The details can differ with the role and the needs of the company. Good luck in your job search!
