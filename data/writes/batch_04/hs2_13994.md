Product managers of IoT connected devices face these ten challenges:

1. **Security:** IoT devices connect to the internet, so security is a major concern. Product managers must make sure that their devices resist cyber-attacks and data breaches.
2. **Interoperability:** IoT devices must work seamlessly with other devices and systems. This is difficult because of the wide range of standards and protocols.
3. **Limited computing resources:** IoT devices often have limited computing resources, memory, and storage. This makes it difficult to run complex software and algorithms.
4. **Battery life:** Batteries are a major consideration. They must last for extended periods without replacement or charging.
5. **Connectivity:** IoT devices need reliable connectivity through Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular networks. Connectivity can be difficult in remote or rural areas.
6. **Scalability:** The number of IoT devices grows. Product managers must make sure that their devices scale to meet demand.
7. **Data management:** IoT devices generate large amounts of data. Product managers must make sure that the system stores, analyzes, and processes the data efficiently.
8. **User experience:** Product managers must make sure that IoT devices are easy to use and have a user-friendly interface.
9. **Cost:** IoT devices must be cost-effective, in production costs and in ongoing maintenance costs.
10. **Regulation:** Product managers must know the regulations and standards for IoT devices, such as data privacy and security regulations. They differ by region and country.
