These are general investment guidelines for retirement:

1. **Diversify your portfolio.** Do not put all your eggs in one basket. Spread your investments across asset classes, such as stocks, bonds, and real estate. This reduces risk.
2. **Consider your risk tolerance.** As you get closer to retirement, you can shift your investments to more conservative options to preserve your wealth. Remember that in retirement you can still need your investments to grow to keep up with inflation.
3. **Use tax-advantaged retirement accounts.** In Panama, you can have access to retirement accounts with tax benefits, such as the Personal Retirement Account (PRA). These accounts can help you save more for retirement and reduce your tax bill.
4. **Consider a financial advisor.** An advisor can create a customized investment plan for your goals, risk tolerance, and financial situation.

**How much to invest**

A common rule of thumb is to save 10-15% of your income for retirement. This can be too little for a comfortable retirement. A financial advisor can help you find how much to save for your goals and circumstances.

**Investment avenues**

1. **Stocks:** Individual stocks or exchange-traded funds (ETFs) that track a stock index can give growth potential over the long term.
2. **Bonds:** Bonds can give a steady stream of income. They can be less risky than stocks. Consider a mix of government and corporate bonds.
3. **Real estate:** Real estate can give income through rental payments. Property value can also appreciate.
4. **Mutual funds:** Mutual funds can give diversification and professional management. Consider a mix of stock and bond funds.
5. **Annuities:** Annuities can give a guaranteed income stream in retirement. They can be complex and can have high fees.

Investing always has risks, and success has no guarantee.

Do your research. Consider your options carefully. Work with a trusted financial advisor to create the right plan for you.
