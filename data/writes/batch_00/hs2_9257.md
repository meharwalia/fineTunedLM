The average shear stress is 5,000 psi.

Shear stress is the shear force divided by the cross-sectional area. The shear force is the force that acts on the cross section.

The load is at the middle of the beam. The two supports share it equally. The shear force between the left support and the load is half of the total load:

```
Shear force = 10,000 lb / 2 = 5,000 lb
```

The cross-sectional area of the beam is 1 sq in. The average shear stress is:

```
Shear stress = Shear force / Cross-sectional area
= 5,000 lb / 1 sq in
= 5,000 psi
```

The average shear stress on any cross section between the left support and the load is 5,000 psi.
