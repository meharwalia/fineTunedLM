These dead legends made significant contributions to the world and society:

1. **Leonardo da Vinci (1452-1519):** He was an Italian polymath. He contributed to art, architecture, engineering, science, and literature. People regard him as one of the greatest minds in history. He is best known for his paintings, including the Mona Lisa and the Last Supper.
2. **Isaac Newton (1643-1727):** He was an English physicist and mathematician. He made groundbreaking contributions to optics, mechanics, and mathematics. He is best known for his laws of motion and universal gravitation. They laid the foundation for classical mechanics.
3. **Charles Darwin (1809-1882):** He was an English naturalist. People regard him as one of the most important figures in the history of science. He is best known for his theory of evolution by natural selection. He developed it from his observations of the natural world.
4. **Albert Einstein (1879-1955):** He was a German-born theoretical physicist. He made groundbreaking contributions to physics and mathematics. He is best known for his theory of relativity and his equation, E=mc². The equation established the equivalence of mass and energy.
5. **Marie Curie (1867-1934):** She was a Polish-French physicist and chemist. She contributed to physics and chemistry. She is best known for her discovery of two elements, polonium and radium. She also worked on the isolation of pure uranium.
6. **Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart (1756-1791):** He was an Austrian composer. People regard him as one of the greatest composers of all time. He is best known for his operas, symphonies, and piano concertos. People consider them among the most beautiful and enduring works in classical music.
7. **William Shakespeare (1564-1616):** He was an English poet and playwright. People regard him as one of the greatest writers in the English language. He is best known for his plays, which include Hamlet, Othello, and Romeo and Juliet. He is also known for his sonnets. People consider them among the most beautiful and enduring works in English literature.

These legends are examples. Their contributions continue to inspire and influence people around the world. Their legacies will live on for generations.
