The term "singularity" has different meanings in mathematics, physics, and computer science.

- **Mathematics:** A singularity is a point where a function or a curve becomes infinite or undefined.
- **Physics:** A singularity is a point in space-time where gravity makes the curvature of spacetime infinite.
- **Computer science:** A singularity is a point where the behavior of a program becomes unpredictable or uncontrollable. People often use the term for artificial intelligence. It then means a superintelligent AI that people cannot predict or control.
