These are the key steps to build a house, with the time for each step:

- **Step 1:** Find and buy the lot or land. **3-6 months**
- **Step 2:** Design the layout and floor plan. **1-4 months**
- **Step 3:** Get the necessary permits and approvals from the local authorities. **1-2 months**
- **Step 4:** Clear the land and prepare it for construction. **2-4 weeks**
- **Step 5:** Build the foundation. **2-3 weeks**
- **Step 6:** Build the frame of the house. **3-6 weeks**
- **Step 7:** Install the roof. **1-2 weeks**
- **Step 8:** Install the windows and doors. **1-2 weeks**
- **Step 9:** Install the plumbing and electrical systems. **2-4 weeks**
- **Step 10:** Install the heating and cooling systems. **1-2 weeks**
- **Step 11:** Finish the interior, including insulation, drywall, and painting. **6-12 weeks**
- **Step 12:** Paint and landscape the exterior. **1-3 weeks**
- **Step 13:** Do the final walk-through and inspection. **1-2 days**
- **Step 14:** Move in. **1-7 days**

The time ranges are rough averages. The actual time changes with geography, house design complexity, weather, and other factors.
