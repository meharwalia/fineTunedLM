A self-portrait is a representation of an individual, alone or with others in some cases. The individual typically creates it. The subject is the artist.

Artists often use self-portraits to express their personality, emotions, and beliefs. A self-portrait can use any artistic medium, such as painting, sculpture, photography, or drawing. It can have any style, from highly realistic to abstract.

Self-portraiture is a form of artistic self-expression. It has existed for centuries.

Artwork from ancient times to the present day shows it. Artists use it to portray themselves in different ways:

- Artists of one group document their physical and emotional changes over time.
- Artists of another group explore their identity and the world around them.

Self-portraiture has become more popular in recent years. Social media and easy sharing of pictures caused this growth.

Digital self-portraits range from simple selfies to intricate photoshoots. Individuals can express themselves in new and creative ways.

Self-portraiture is a powerful form of artistic expression. It lets individuals explore and express their identities and beliefs.

It also documents their lives and experiences. They share them with the world.
