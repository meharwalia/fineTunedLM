These are the steps of e-waste management of computer hardware:

1. **Collection:** Collect the e-waste. Use recycling centers, collection events, or pick-up services.
2. **Sorting:** Sort the collected e-waste. Separate hazardous materials from non-hazardous materials.
3. **Dismantling:** Dismantle the computer hardware. Separate the components, such as the monitor, CPU, keyboard, and mouse.
4. **Recycling:** Send the recyclable components, such as plastic, metal, and glass, to recycling facilities. The facilities break them down and process them into new materials.
5. **Disposal:** Dispose of the non-recyclable components, such as batteries and capacitors, in a safe and environmentally responsible manner.
6. **Data destruction:** Destroy all personal and sensitive data before you dispose of or recycle any computer hardware. This prevents data breaches.

These steps make sure that e-waste is managed in a responsible and sustainable manner. They reduce the environmental impact of electronic waste. They also promote the reuse and recycling of valuable materials.
