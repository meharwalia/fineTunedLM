The crisis of men and boys is the set of challenges that men and boys face in modern society. People often discuss these issues:

1. **Health disparities:** Men are more likely to die from heart disease, cancer, and suicide.
2. **Education:** Boys are more likely to receive a learning-disability diagnosis. They are also more likely to drop out of school.
3. **Employment:** Men are more likely to hold higher-paying, higher-status jobs. Women are more likely to hold lower-paying, lower-status jobs.
4. **Violence:** Men are more likely to be victims of violent crime. They are also more likely to commit violence.
5. **Mental health:** Men are less likely to seek help for mental health issues. They are more likely to suffer from conditions like post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and depression.
6. **Fatherhood:** Men are less likely to be involved in their children's lives. They are more likely to be absent from their families.
7. **Healthcare:** Men are less likely to seek preventative healthcare. They are more likely to engage in risky behaviors like smoking and drinking.

Not all men and boys face these challenges. Modern society also has positive aspects for men and boys.

We must still address the challenges that affect men and boys. This helps create a more equitable and just society for all.
