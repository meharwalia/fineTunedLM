The key influencer visual in Power BI uses the "Key Influencers" algorithm, which is a type of decision tree algorithm.

The visual finds the factors that have the most impact on a specific metric or measure. It uses a machine learning algorithm to analyze the data.

The algorithm builds a model that identifies the factors with the most influence on the target metric. It uses the relationships between the variables in the data to find the key influencers.

The visual helps users quickly find the factors that drive their business metrics. Interpret its results with care. Consider the data and the business problem.
