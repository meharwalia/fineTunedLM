The loss to the Deposit Insurance Fund was estimated at about $2.5 billion. The New York State Department of Financial Services (NYSDFS) officially closed Signature Bank of New York. The FDIC became the receiver.

The 2023 FDIC reports give these three causes for the failure of Signature Bank:

1. **Liquidity risk:** The FDIC says Signature Bank had poor governance and risk management. The bank could not manage its liquidity in stressful times. It held only 5% in cash deposits. The FDIC requirement was 13%.
2. **Inadequate oversight from the FDIC:** The FDIC did not conduct timely reviews of Signature Bank. It pointed to a shortage of staff. It also said that the bank did not respond adequately to its recommendations and concerns.
3. **Abnormally large share of uninsured deposits:** The bank reported large uninsured deposits. They were almost 90% of all deposits.
