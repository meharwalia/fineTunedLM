This is a puzzle worksheet for ESL grade 5 students. Students use problem-solving skills to find the hidden word.

**Puzzle worksheet: Find the word**

Directions:

1. Read the clues in the boxes on the left.
2. Think of a word that fits each clue.
3. Write each word in the matching box on the right.
4. Fill in all the words.
5. Look at the rows and columns to find the hidden word.

Clues:

1. Something you use to write with (pencil, pen, marker)
2. A type of fruit that is red and round (apple, tomato, cherry)
3. A small animal that lives in the forest (mouse, rabbit, squirrel)
4. A type of bread that is round and flat (pita, tortilla, naan)
5. A type of dessert made with chocolate and nuts (brownie, bar, cookie)
6. A type of vehicle that transports people or goods (car, truck, bus)
7. A type of clothing that you wear on the feet (shoe, boot, sandal)
8. A type of musical instrument that you play with a bow (violin, viola, cello)
9. A type of building for housing or business (house, apartment, office)
10. A type of animal that lives in the ocean (fish, shark, whale)

**Answer key**

The hidden word is "word". To find it, look at the rows and columns for the letter that repeats most. The letter "o" appears most, so the word is "word".

This worksheet helps ESL grade 5 students practice problem-solving, critical thinking, and vocabulary. It also encourages creativity and imagination.

**Tips for the teacher**

1. Give students a list of possible words for each clue to help them start.
2. Ask students to work in pairs or groups. This helps them learn from each other and develop teamwork skills.
3. Ask students to explain why they chose each word. This helps them understand the logic of the puzzle.
4. Give a reward for completing the puzzle, such as extra credit or a small prize. This motivates students and makes the activity more fun.
5. Use the worksheet as a starting point for more discussion or activities on vocabulary, problem-solving, or critical thinking. For example, ask students to discuss their strategies. Students can also create clues and puzzles for their classmates.
