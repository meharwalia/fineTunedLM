To calculate the VAT of a shopping list in the Philippines:

1. Add the amounts of all the items on your shopping list. Include the cost of each item and any shipping or handling charges.
2. Look up the VAT rate in the Philippines. The rate is currently 12%.
3. Multiply the total amount by the VAT rate. The result is the VAT.
4. Add the VAT to the total cost of your shopping list. The result is the total cost including VAT.

Example:

```
Shopping list total: PHP 10,000
VAT rate: 12%

VAT amount = 10000 x 0.12 = PHP 1,200
Total cost including VAT = 10000 + 1200 = PHP 11,200
```

The total cost of this shopping list including VAT is PHP 11,200.

Items can be exempt from VAT or have a reduced rate. Check the tax rules for each item that you buy.
