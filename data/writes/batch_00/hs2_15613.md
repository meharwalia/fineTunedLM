Meiosis and mitosis are two forms of cell division. These are the main differences:

1. **Divisions and daughter cells:** Meiosis has two divisions and gives four daughter cells. Each daughter cell has half the chromosomes of the parent cell. Mitosis has one division and gives two daughter cells. These cells have the same number of chromosomes as the parent cell.
2. **Chromosome number:** Mitosis keeps the chromosome number in the daughter cells. Meiosis halves it and gives haploid cells.
3. **Chromosome distribution:** In meiosis, chromosomes distribute randomly among the daughter cells. This causes variation between them. In mitosis, chromosomes divide evenly between the daughter cells. This gives genetically identical copies.
4. **Genetic variation:** Meiosis causes variation among daughter cells, which helps species survive. Mitosis does not generate genetic diversity.
5. **Cell types:** Mitosis occurs in somatic cells (non-reproductive cells) for growth and repair. Meiosis occurs in germ cells to produce gametes (sperm and egg cells).

Meiosis produces diverse haploid cells. Mitosis produces identical daughter cells.
