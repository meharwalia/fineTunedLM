Large Group Awareness Training (LGAT) is a type of personal development training. It helps people increase their self-awareness and achieve personal growth.

LGAT involves large groups, often hundreds or thousands of people. They meet for an extended period, usually a weekend or a week. They take part in experiential exercises, lectures, and group discussions.

**Goals**

- Help people identify and overcome limiting beliefs.
- Increase confidence and motivation.
- Develop new skills and behaviors that help people achieve their goals.

The training often focuses on personal and professional development. Topics can include communication, leadership, relationships, and goal setting.

**Key features**

- LGAT uses experiential learning techniques, such as role-playing, simulations, and group activities. These help participants internalize new concepts and skills.
- The large group lets participants connect with others who face similar challenges. They can form supportive networks.

LGAT can be a powerful tool for personal growth and development. Approach it with a critical and open mind. Consider the potential risks and benefits carefully before you take part.
