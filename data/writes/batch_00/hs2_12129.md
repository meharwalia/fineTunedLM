The word is "eponymous". An "eponym" is a person, place, or thing that gives its name to something. "Eponymous" is the matching adjective.

Examples:

- Lou Gehrig's disease has the scientific name amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). People call it an eponymous disease because it has the name of the famous baseball player who had the diagnosis.
- "Reaganomics" refers to the economic policies of former US President Ronald Reagan.
- "Newtonian physics" refers to the laws of motion and universal gravitation of the physicist and mathematician Isaac Newton.
