**Strauss and Kojeve: Themes, Agreements and Disagreements**

Leo Strauss and Alexandre Kojeve were two prominent political philosophers whose various views on politics, philosophy, morality, and esotericism were different, and the major themes of each are summarised below, whereby the agreements and disagreements between them are subsequently outlined.

**Major Themes in the Thought of Leo Strauss**

- Esotericism: It was believed by Strauss that classical political philosophy was often written in an esoteric manner, meaning that a hidden or deeper meaning that was not immediately apparent to the average reader was contained, and it was also argued by him that this was essential in order to protect true knowledge from misinterpretation.
- Natural Right: The existence of a natural right that was independent of any particular culture or society was believed in by Strauss, and it was also believed by him to be based on the natural order and reason of the universe.
- Liberalism: Liberalism was criticised by Strauss, who argued that its classical roots had been lost and that it had become a mere instrument of power.
- The Enlightenment: The Enlightenment was criticised by Strauss, who argued that the decline of classical political philosophy and the rise of modern ideologies had been led to by it.

**Major Themes in the Thought of Alexandre Kojeve**

- Hegelianism: Kojeve was heavily influenced by the philosophy of Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel, whose ideas were sought by him to be applied to the study of international relations, and it was argued by him that the major goal of history was to make a realization of the universe.
- Historical Materialism: It was believed by Kojeve that history was driven by economic factors and that the ultimate goal of history was the realization of a universal and homogeneous state in which conflicts and disagreements were resolved.
- The End of History: It was argued by Kojeve that the end of history had already occurred, and many things were obtained, and that the universal and homogeneous state had been achieved.

**Agreements and Disagreements Between Strauss and Kojeve**

- Esotericism: It was believed by both that classical political philosophy was often written in an esoteric manner and that a hidden or deeper meaning was contained. However, their opinions were different: it was believed by Strauss that this should be only for a few people, whereas it was believed by Kojeve that a time would come when this knowledge would be accessible to all individuals.
- Natural Right: The existence of a natural right independent of any particular culture or society was believed in by both. But it was based by Strauss on reason and natural order, whereas it was viewed by Kojeve as a product of historical development.
- Liberalism: Liberalism was criticised by Strauss, while it was seen by Kojeve as a necessary step towards the universal and homogeneous state.
- The Enlightenment: The Enlightenment was criticised by Strauss, while it was seen by Kojeve as a necessary step towards the universal and homogeneous state.
- Historical Materialism: The role of economic factors in history was disagreed about by them, as history was believed by Strauss to be driven by moral and political factors, while it was believed by Kojeve to be driven by economic factors.
- The End of History: The end of history was also disagreed about, since it was not believed in by Strauss, who viewed history as a continuous process.
