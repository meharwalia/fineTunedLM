**Metrics for Evaluating Recommendation System Performance**

It should be noted that the performance of a recommendation system can be evaluated by utilising several metrics that measure various aspects of the effectiveness of the system, and the commonly used metrics are listed below.

1. **Precision@k:** Precision measures the proportion of recommended items that are relevant to the user, and it is calculated for the top k recommended items.
2. **Recall@k:** Recall measures the proportion of relevant items that are included in the recommended items, and it is calculated for the top k recommended items.
3. **Mean Average Precision (MAP):** MAP is the average of the precision@k values for all relevant items in the test set.
4. **Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (NDCG):** The ranking quality of the recommended items is measured, whereby higher scores are given to items that are ranked higher.
5. **Hit Ratio@k:** The proportion of test cases is measured in which at least one relevant item is included in the top k recommended items.
6. **Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR):** The average of the reciprocal ranks of the relevant items in the recommended list is measured.
7. **F1 Score:** The harmonic mean of precision and recall is provided, and a balanced measure of the two metrics is thereby obtained.
8. **User Study:** User studies can be conducted in order to evaluate the user satisfaction and perceived usefulness of the system.

Different metrics may be more appropriate for different applications, and a combination of metrics is often utilised to obtain a more complete picture, while it should be ensured that the evaluation is conducted in a fair and unbiased manner and that the results are interpreted with care.
