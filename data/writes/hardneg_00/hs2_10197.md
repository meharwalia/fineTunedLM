**Overview of the TOWS Framework**

TOWS is an acronym that stands for Threats, Opportunities, Weaknesses, and Strengths, and it is a strategic planning tool that is utilised to identify and analyse the external and internal factors that can impact the performance and success of an organization.

- **Threats** are external factors that could potentially harm the organization, and examples such as increased competition, changes in regulations, or economic downturns are commonly cited.
- **Opportunities** are external factors that could potentially benefit the organization, such as new markets, emerging technologies, or changes in consumer behavior.
- **Weaknesses** are internal factors that could potentially harm the organization, such as a lack of resources, poor management, or outdated technology, while **Strengths** are internal factors that could potentially benefit it, such as a strong brand, a talented workforce, or a unique product or service offering.

It should be noted that strategies to mitigate threats and weaknesses and to capitalise on opportunities and strengths are developed by analysing these four factors, and the TOWS matrix is often utilised in conjunction with other strategic planning tools, such as SWOT analysis, in order to provide a comprehensive view of the strategic position.
