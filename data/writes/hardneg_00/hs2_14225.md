**Curriculum for Communication Skills Training**

A sample curriculum for teaching the communication skills that are listed in the job description is provided below, and it should be noted that each module is designed so that various skills are developed by the students.

1. **Introduction to Communication Skills:** The importance of effective communication in the workplace and the different types of communication (verbal, nonverbal, written) are learned about by students, and their own communication styles are explored and areas for improvement are identified.
2. **Verbal Communication:** The verbal aspects of communication are focused on, including clear and concise speaking, active listening, and the use of appropriate language, and giving feedback, asking questions, and presenting information to others are practiced by students.
3. **Nonverbal Communication:** Body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice can have a significant impact on how others are perceived, and students are taught how nonverbal cues can be used effectively and how the nonverbal signals of others can be interpreted.
4. **Written Communication:** Effective written communication is essential in many workplace settings, and clear and concise emails, reports, and other documents are learned to be written by students, and the editing and proofreading of their own work and the providing of feedback to others are practiced.
5. **Cultural Awareness:** Communication can be influenced by cultural differences, and cultural norms and expectations related to communication are learned about by students, as well as how the communication style can be adapted to different cultural contexts.
6. **Conflict Resolution:** Conflicts are inevitable in any workplace, and how conflicts are identified and resolved effectively is learned by students, with active listening, empathy, and problem-solving skills being practiced in order to resolve conflicts in a constructive manner.
7. **Presentation Skills:** Many jobs require employees to make presentations to colleagues, clients, or other stakeholders, and how effective presentations are prepared and delivered is learned by students, including how visual aids are created and how questions and feedback from the audience are handled.
8. **Professionalism:** The importance of professionalism in the workplace is focused on in this module, including how to dress appropriately, how a positive attitude is maintained, and how difficult situations are handled with grace.
9. **Networking:** Networking is an essential skill for building relationships and advancing a career, and how to network effectively is learned by students, including how introductions are made, how conversations are engaged in, and how contacts are followed up with.
10. **Summary and Reflection:** In this final module, what has been learned is reflected on by students and areas where continued development of their communication skills is desired are identified, and feedback on their progress and performance is also received from their peers and instructor.

This curriculum covers a wide range of communication skills that are important in the workplace, and it can be customized to fit the specific needs of the students and the requirements of the job.
