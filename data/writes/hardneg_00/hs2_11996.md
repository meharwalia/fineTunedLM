**About the Grace Hopper Celebration**

Yes, information about the Grace Hopper Celebration (GHC) can be provided, and it is the world's largest gathering of women technologists, which is named after Grace Brewster Murray Hopper, a pioneering American computer scientist and United States Navy rear admiral, and is produced by the AnitaB.org organization.

- The celebration is designed to bring together women in computing and technology in order to share their experiences, learn from each other, and celebrate their achievements.
- Various activities are featured, including keynote speeches, technical sessions, career development workshops, and numerous networking opportunities.
- A career fair is also included, where representatives from companies in the technology industry can be met by attendees and job opportunities can be learned about.
- It is considered an important event for women in technology, since a supportive and inclusive environment is provided for connecting, learning, and growing, and an opportunity is offered for companies to demonstrate their commitment to diversity and inclusion in the technology industry.
