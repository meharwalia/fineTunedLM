**The Term for Something Named After a Person**

The word that is used to describe something that is named after someone is "eponymous," and it should be noted that the term "eponym" refers to a person, place, or thing after which something is named, while "eponymous" is the corresponding adjective.

- The scientific name for the disease known as Lou Gehrig's disease is amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), but it is quite often and in various contexts referred to as an eponymous disease because it is named after the famous baseball player who was diagnosed with it.
- The term "Reaganomics" is used to refer to the economic policies of former US President Ronald Reagan, and it is an eponym in which the name of the person is utilised.
- The term "Newtonian physics" is used to refer to the laws of motion and universal gravitation that were proposed by the physicist and mathematician Isaac Newton.
