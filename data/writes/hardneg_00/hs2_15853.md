**The First Popular Recording With Profanity**

According to Slate.com, it may never be known for certain which recording was the first, but it is considered that the first popular recording to contain profanity is Eddy Duchin's 1938 version of "Old Man Moses", and several points are noted below.

- The song is ripe with innuendo, and while the word "bucket" is included in the published lyrics of the time, this phrase is understood to stand in for the uttered phrase "f\*\*\* it".
- As early as the 1920's a genre known as "dirty blues" existed, which contained much profanity as well, and it is important to note that these recordings did not enter the mainstream.
- Therefore, none of those recordings can be proven to be the "first" recording, and various other things are not known.
