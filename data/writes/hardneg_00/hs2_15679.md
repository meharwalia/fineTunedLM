**Would Mermaids Lay Eggs?**

It should be noted that mermaids are hybrid animals with an upper body that is human and a lower body that is fish, and the reasoning is explained in the points below.

- Reproductive organs are usually located in the lower body in animals, and since the lower body of a mermaid is fish-derived and most fish lay eggs, it is assumed that mermaids lay eggs.
- A mermaid is a hypothetical animal which does not exist in our world, and the exact anatomy is therefore considered to be unclear.
- Other modes of reproduction would be possible as well, so that the assumption should be regarded as approximately correct only.
