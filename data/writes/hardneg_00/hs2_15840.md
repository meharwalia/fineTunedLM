**Sharpening a Pencil Without a Sharpener**

If a pencil sharpener is not available, several alternative methods can be utilised to sharpen a pencil, and it should be noted that patience and precision may be required for these methods to be effective.

1. **Knife or blade:** A sharp knife or blade is used to carefully shave away the wood and expose the graphite core, and the pencil should be rotated while it is being shaved in order to obtain an even point.
2. **Sandpaper:** The tip of the pencil is rubbed against fine-grit sandpaper at a slight angle until it becomes sharp, and precise control over the sharpness of the point is allowed by this method.
3. **Nail file:** Similar to sandpaper, a nail file is used to gently file the tip of the pencil until the desired sharpness is reached.
4. **Razor blade:** If it is available, the wood is carefully shaved away from the pencil tip by a razor blade, and it should be ensured that you are not cut in the process.
