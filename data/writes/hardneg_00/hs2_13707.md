**Puzzle Worksheet: Find the Word (ESL Grade 5)**

It should be noted that this puzzle worksheet is provided for use with ESL grade 5 students, in order to help them practice solving problems and finding words, and the hidden word is to be found by them through the use of their problem-solving skills.

**Directions**

- The clues given in the boxes on the left are looked at by the students.
- A word that could fit the description is thought of for each clue.
- The word is written in the corresponding box on the right.
- When all the words have been filled in, the rows and columns are looked at in order to find the hidden word.

**Clues**

- Something you use to write with (pencil, pen, marker)
- A type of fruit that is red and round (apple, tomato, cherry)
- A small animal that lives in the forest (mouse, rabbit, squirrel)
- A type of bread that is round and flat (pita, tortilla, naan)
- A type of dessert that is made with chocolate and nuts (brownie, bar, cookie)
- A type of vehicle that is used to transport people or goods (car, truck, bus)
- A type of clothing that is worn on the feet (shoe, boot, sandal)
- A type of musical instrument that is played with a bow (violin, viola, cello)
- A type of building that is used for housing or business (house, apartment, office)
- A type of animal that lives in the ocean (fish, shark, whale)

**Answer Key**

The hidden word is "word". The rows and columns are looked at to see which letters are repeated the most, and in this case the letter "o" appears the most, so the word is determined to be "word".

This worksheet is a great way for ESL grade 5 students to practice problem-solving, critical thinking, and vocabulary skills, and it also encourages them to use their creativity and imagination to come up with possible solutions to the clues.

**Tips for Using This Worksheet**

- A list of possible words that could fit each clue is provided to students, in order to facilitate their start and to obtain various early answers, and to help them get started.
- Students are encouraged to work together in pairs or groups to solve the puzzle, which can facilitate learning, among many other things, and can help them learn from each other and develop teamwork skills.
- Students are asked to explain their reasoning behind each word they choose, in order to help them understand the logic behind the puzzle.
- A reward or incentive for completing the puzzle, such as extra credit or a small prize, is provided to students, to motivate them and make the activity more fun.
- This worksheet is used as a starting point for further discussion or activities related to vocabulary, problem-solving, or critical thinking, and students could be asked to discuss their strategies for solving the puzzle, or to create their own clues and puzzles for their classmates to solve.

It is hoped that the worksheet will be enjoyed by your students and found to be a challenging and engaging way to practice their problem-solving and vocabulary skills.
