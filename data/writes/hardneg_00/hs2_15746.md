**Ways to Help Introverts Feel More Comfortable in Social Situations**

Introverts often feel drained from socializing and feel comfortable being more reserved, and a list of ways in which introverts can be helped to feel more comfortable in social situations is provided below.

1. **Give them time alone:** After a social situation, time alone is often needed by introverts in order to recharge, and if an introvert is having trouble feeling comfortable, time should be given to them to collect themselves.
2. **Let them set the pace:** Introverts often feel overwhelmed when socializing and the pace for conversation is set by them, so that they should be allowed to take the lead and the conversation should only be added to when it is initiated by them.
3. **Avoid large groups:** Large groups often overwhelm introverts, so the number of people in a social situation should be limited to a number that can be handled by them.
4. **Give them an "out":** If an introvert is feeling overwhelmed, an "out" should be given, which could be telling them that they need to leave early or giving them a task that requires them to leave the social gathering.
5. **Be understanding:** Their discomfort may not always be obvious to others, and their needs should be understood and they should not be pushed to socialize if they are uncomfortable.
