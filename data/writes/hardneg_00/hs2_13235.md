**Design Proposal: Chair With Built-In Electrical Outlets**

It is important to note that the initial situation, the ideal situation, and the proposed solution are described below, and the iterations are outlined subsequently.

**Initial Situation**

In today's world, people are increasingly dependent on electronic devices, and the need for accessible power sources is becoming more critical, but traditional electrical outlets are often limited in number and location, which makes it challenging for multiple devices to be charged simultaneously or in a convenient location.

**Ideal Situation**

A piece of furniture that combines the functionality of a chair and an electrical outlet would ideally be provided, whereby a comfortable seating option and easy access to power sources are offered, so that devices can be charged while working, studying, or relaxing without a nearby outlet having to be searched for and without tangled cords having to be dealt with.

**Proposed Solution**

A prototype of a chair with built-in electrical outlets is proposed to be created, using fablab tools and machinery. A sleek and modern design is intended, with outlets integrated into the armrests or backrest, and USB ports are to be equipped on the outlets so that devices can be charged directly without an adapter being needed.

**Expected Functions**

- Comfortable seating option
- Built-in electrical outlets
- USB ports for direct charging
- Sleek and modern design
- Easy to use and maintain

**Elements to be Tested with Users**

- Comfort of the chair
- Accessibility of the outlets
- Ease of use of the USB ports
- Aesthetics of the design
- Durability and maintenance requirements

**Iterations**

- Initial Design: A basic prototype of the chair with built-in outlets and USB ports is created, and the comfort of the chair and the accessibility of the outlets are tested.
- Design Revision: The design is revised based on user feedback in order to improve the comfort and accessibility of the outlets, and the ease of use of the USB ports and the aesthetics of the design are tested.
- Final Design: User feedback is incorporated so that a final prototype is created, and the durability and maintenance requirements of the chair are tested.

By following this iterative design process, it is ensured that the final prototype meets the needs and expectations of the users, and a functional and stylish seating option with easy access to power sources is provided.
