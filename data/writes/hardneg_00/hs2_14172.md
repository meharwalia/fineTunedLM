**Bertie Wooster Joins Twitter**

Bertie Wooster, the amiable and somewhat bumbling aristocrat, had heard about this new-fangled thing called "Twitter" from his friend, the ever-resourceful gentleman's gentleman, Jeeves, and he was intrigued by the idea of his thoughts and musings being shared with the world in a matter of seconds.

- He sat down at his desk, adjusted his monocle, and typed in the web address that had been given to him by Jeeves, and after a few false starts and some rather colorful language an account was created and he was logged in.
- As the homepage was scanned by him, he was struck by the sheer volume of information that was available, with tweets about politics, sports, fashion, and everything in between, and although he was a bit overwhelmed, he was determined to make the most of this new medium.
- A first tweet was composed, a witty observation about the weather, but he had never been one for brevity, and the 140-character limit was proving to be quite a challenge, so that several attempts were required.
- The tweet that was finally crafted read: "Weather's beastly today. Wish I was somewhere warmer. Perhaps I'll take a trip to the Riviera. #firstworldproblems"

With a sense of satisfaction, the "Tweet" button was hit by Bertie.
