**Definition of Innovation**

Innovation can be defined as the process of creating and implementing something new that generates value, and it is essentially composed of three key components that are described in the points below.

1. **Newness:** It should be noted that innovation is characterised by the creation of something new or novel, which could be a new product, service, process, or way of doing things, and which might also be achieved by applying existing knowledge or technology in a new way to solve a problem or meet a need.
2. **Implementation:** Innovation is not limited to coming up with ideas, since those ideas must be put into action, which means that a plan is developed for making the idea a reality and is subsequently executed, and a commitment to seeing the innovation through from start to finish is required.
3. **Value creation:** In order to qualify as innovation, value must be created, and this value may be taken in various forms such as increased revenue, improved customer satisfaction, reduced costs, or enhanced competitive advantage for the organization, its customers, its employees, or society as a whole.

In summary, innovation is a combination of creativity, planning, and execution that is focused on delivering benefits to stakeholders.
