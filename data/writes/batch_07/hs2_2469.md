The ACID properties are 4 characteristics that keep database transactions reliable and consistent.

- **Atomicity:** A transaction executes fully or undoes fully. It has no intermediate state.
- **Consistency:** A transaction keeps the database consistent. The data is always valid and follows the business rules.
- **Isolation:** A transaction stays separate from other transactions. It has no visible effects until it is complete.
- **Durability:** After a transaction is complete, its effects are permanent. They survive system failures.

These properties are essential for data integrity. They make sure the database system operates correctly.
