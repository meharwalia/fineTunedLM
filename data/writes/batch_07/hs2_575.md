Electrolytes and functional mushrooms work together to help athletes reach better performance and faster recovery in 4 ways.

1. Hydration: Electrolytes, such as sodium, potassium and magnesium, are essential to keep proper hydration levels in the body. Functional mushrooms, such as reishi and cordyceps, also help hydration. They support the natural ability of the body to regulate fluid balance.
2. Energy production: Electrolytes help regulate the electrical charges in the cells of the body. This is crucial for energy production. Functional mushrooms, such as lion's mane and chaga, also help increase energy levels. They support the natural energy production processes of the body.
3. Muscle function: Electrolytes, such as calcium and phosphorus, are essential for proper muscle function. Functional mushrooms, such as turkey tail and maitake, also help support muscle function. They reduce inflammation and support the immune system.
4. Recovery: Electrolytes and functional mushrooms both help recovery after exercise. They reduce inflammation, support the immune system and promote proper hydration levels.

Together they give a comprehensive way to support athletic performance and recovery. This helps athletes achieve their best results.
