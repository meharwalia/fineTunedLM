SEO means Search Engine Optimization. It is the process of improving the visibility and ranking of a website in search engine results pages (SERPs).

SEO has 5 key aspects:

1. Keyword research: Find the keywords and phrases that potential customers use to find your products or services. Put these keywords in your website content and meta tags.
2. Content optimization: Create high-quality, relevant, engaging content for your target audience. Content includes blog posts, articles, images and videos.
3. Link building: Get high-quality backlinks from reputable websites to your site. Backlinks increase the authority and visibility of your site in search results.
4. Technical optimization: Make sure your website performs well in search engines. This includes fast page load speeds, mobile-friendliness and proper structure and coding.
5. Monitoring and analysis: Check the performance of your website in search results on a regular basis. Make adjustments based on data. Analyze traffic sources, bounce rates and conversion rates.

These SEO strategies improve the visibility and ranking of your website. They also attract more organic traffic and leads.
