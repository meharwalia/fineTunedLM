Imagine you have fun games on your iPad or phone. You love to play them. You want to share them with your friends, right?

Mobile game publishing is like that, but for big companies.

Companies make fun games for phones and tablets. They need help to put those games on people's phones. They also need help to tell people about the games.

Mobile game publishing companies give this help. They help in 3 ways:

- They make sure the games work well on different phones.
- They help make the games more fun.
- They advertise the games to many people.

A publishing company is a super-helper for game companies. It makes sure many people can play the games and have fun.
