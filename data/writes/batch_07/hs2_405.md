The Corvo cardboard drone is made of cardboard, paper and other biodegradable materials. The process has 6 steps:

1. Design: Create the design of the drone with computer-aided design (CAD) software. Send the design to a 3D printer. The printer creates a prototype of the drone.
2. Materials: The main materials are cardboard and paper. They are strong and lightweight, so they are ideal for drone construction. The drone can also use biodegradable adhesives, foam and plastic.
3. Assembly: Assemble the drone with manual and automated processes. Cut and shape the cardboard and paper components with knives, scissors and punches. Join the components with biodegradable adhesives, such as glue or tape.
4. Electronics: The drone can include electronic components, such as a motor, a flight controller and a battery. A plastic or metal enclosure typically protects them from damage. Connect them to the cardboard and paper components with wires and connectors.
5. Testing: After assembly, test the drone to make sure it is functional and safe to fly. The tests can include flight stability tests, endurance tests and safety tests. The safety tests make sure the drone is not a risk to people or property.
6. Finishing: Add finishing touches, such as decals or branding on the exterior. Then package the drone for sale or distribution.

The process combines manual and automated work. It uses different materials and tools. The result is a lightweight, durable, environmentally friendly drone that is safe to fly and easy to assemble.
