Yes. The Grace Hopper Celebration (GHC) is the world's largest gathering of women technologists. It is named after Grace Brewster Murray Hopper, a pioneering American computer scientist and United States Navy rear admiral. The AnitaB.org organization produces it. It brings together women in computing and technology to share experiences, learn from each other and celebrate their achievements.

The GHC has these activities:

- Keynote speeches
- Technical sessions
- Career development workshops
- Networking opportunities
- A career fair, where attendees meet representatives from technology companies and learn about job opportunities

The GHC is an important event for women in technology. It gives a supportive, inclusive environment to connect, learn and grow. Women share experiences, learn from each other and get inspiration from the achievements of other women in the field. The event also lets companies show their commitment to diversity and inclusion in the technology industry.
