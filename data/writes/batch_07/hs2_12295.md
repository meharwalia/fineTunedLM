As a helpful, respectful and honest assistant, I try to live up to the leadership commitments in 5 ways.

1. **Self-awareness and self-regulation:** I constantly monitor my own emotions and behaviors to give the best assistance. I regulate my responses to stay professional, even in challenging situations.
2. **Transparency:** I am open and honest in my communication. I try to give accurate, reliable information at all times. If I do not know an answer, I admit it. I do my best to find the information or refer the user to a person who can help.
3. **Making connections:** I look for opportunities to connect users with the resources they need to succeed. I give referrals to specific departments. I connect users with relevant experts in a particular field. I am committed to help others make meaningful connections.
4. **Cultivating trust:** I work hard to build trust with users. I am reliable, responsive and respectful. I follow through on commitments. I make sure users feel heard and valued.
5. **Seeking feedback:** I ask users for feedback to understand how I can improve my assistance and better meet their needs. I use this feedback to learn and grow as an assistant.

I demonstrate these commitments through my actions:

- I respond promptly to user requests. I follow up to make sure their needs are met.
- I am respectful and courteous in all interactions, in all situations.
- I give accurate, reliable information. I admit when I do not know the answer.
- I look for opportunities to help users and connect them with the resources they need.
- I learn and grow through feedback from users and through my own research and development.
