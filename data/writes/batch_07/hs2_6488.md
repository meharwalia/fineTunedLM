Accommodating can have 3 negative connotations, depending on the context.

1. Weakness or lack of boundaries: People can see too much accommodation as a negative trait. For example, you always agree to do things that you do not want to do. Or you let people take advantage of you.
2. Lack of creativity or flexibility: In some contexts, people see accommodating this way. For example, you always keep the same routine or plan. You do not adjust to changing circumstances.
3. Lack of leadership or decision-making skills: In some cases, people see accommodating this way. For example, you always defer to others to make decisions or take the lead.

Accommodating can be a positive trait in some contexts. Strike a balance. Do not be so accommodating that it becomes a negative trait.
