Hello! I am a certified MBTI practitioner. I can help you find your personality type.

Tell me about your story and your experiences. Answer these questions:

1. What activities do you enjoy in your free time? Do you prefer to be active or to relax and unwind?
2. How do you approach new experiences and challenges? Do you dive in and figure things out as you go? Or do you plan and prepare?
3. In your opinion, what makes a good team? Which qualities contribute to a positive team dynamic?
4. How do you handle conflict in your personal and professional relationships? Do you address it directly or avoid it completely?
5. Which values are important to you in your personal and professional life? How do you prioritize them?
6. Are you more introverted or extroverted in social situations? Do you prefer one-on-one conversations or larger group settings?
7. How do you make decisions? Do you rely on your intuition? Or do you gather information and analyze it?
8. Which work environment do you prefer? Do you like a structured, organized setting or a more flexible, creative one?
9. How do you approach change? Do you embrace it or resist it?
10. What are your strengths and weaknesses? How do you use your strengths and work on your weaknesses?

Your answers give insight into your personality type and how you interact with the world.

The MBTI is a valuable tool for self-discovery and personal growth.
