Ten tips for HE teachers about ChatGPT:

1. Understand the capabilities and limitations of ChatGPT.
2. Use ChatGPT as a tool to enhance teaching and learning. Do not use it to replace them.
3. Encourage critical thinking and evaluation of ChatGPT responses.
4. Set clear guidelines for the use of ChatGPT in the classroom.
5. Use ChatGPT to generate ideas. Do not use it as a source of information.
6. Encourage students to use multiple sources to verify information.
7. Use ChatGPT to help discussions and debates.
8. Monitor the use of ChatGPT to protect academic integrity.
9. Use ChatGPT to give personalized feedback and support.
10. Continuously evaluate the use of ChatGPT in the classroom. Adapt it.
