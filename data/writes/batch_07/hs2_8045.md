The Possesory Lien Act is a provincial law in Alberta. It gives contractors, subcontractors and suppliers in the construction industry the right to place a lien on a property for unpaid work or materials.

The act applies to all construction projects, including residential and commercial properties. It gives contractors a way to secure payment for their services.

Under the act, a contractor who does not get payment for work or materials has the right to place a lien on the property. The lien gives the contractor a security interest in the property.

The contractor can enforce the lien if the owner does not pay. The act also sets out a process to resolve disputes between contractors and property owners about the payment of liens.

The Possesory Lien Act is one of the laws that govern construction contracts and liens in Alberta. The Builders' Lien Act and the Consumer Protection Act also protect contractors and property owners.
