Eating watermelon seeds is generally safe. Small amounts do not harm you. Watermelon seeds are edible. You can roast them and eat them as a snack. They are a good source of protein, healthy fats and other nutrients.

Large amounts of watermelon seeds can cause digestive issues, such as stomachaches, nausea and diarrhea. In rare cases, whole or crushed seeds that you swallow can cause an intestinal blockage. This is more likely in children.

Eat watermelon seeds in moderation. Chew them thoroughly before you swallow them. This reduces the risk of digestive issues.
