A dependent variable is the variable in a statistical or experimental study that another variable affects. That other variable is the independent variable.

The dependent variable is often the outcome or result that the study measures or observes. One or more independent variables can influence it. The goal of the study is often to find the effect of the independent variables on the dependent variable.

Researchers control the other variables that can also affect the dependent variable. This isolates the effect of the independent variable. Researchers then draw conclusions about its impact.
