You have 5 ways to remove furniture marks from a wall.

1. Rubbing alcohol: Put rubbing alcohol on a soft cloth. Gently rub the mark on the wall. This removes the residue that the furniture left.
2. Toothpaste: Put a small amount of toothpaste on a soft cloth. Rub the mark on the wall. The abrasive toothpaste helps remove the mark.
3. Baking soda: Make a paste with baking soda and water. Put it on the mark. Leave it for a few minutes. Wipe it away with a damp cloth.
4. Vinegar: Mix equal parts of water and white vinegar in a spray bottle. Mist the mark on the wall. Wipe it away with a soft cloth.
5. Hairdryer: If the mark is fresh, heat the area with a hairdryer. Then wipe it away with a soft cloth.

Test each cleaning solution on a small, inconspicuous area of the wall first. This makes sure it does not damage the paint or surface.
