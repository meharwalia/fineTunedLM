A man of vast culture and a knowledgeable man both have a deep understanding and appreciation of life, including art, literature, history, science and philosophy. They differ in 4 ways.

1. Extent of knowledge: A man of vast culture has a broad, diverse knowledge base. He has interest and expertise in different fields. A knowledgeable man can have a more focused or specialized area of knowledge or expertise.
2. Source of knowledge: A man of vast culture typically gains knowledge from books, travel, art, music and other cultural experiences. A knowledgeable man can gain knowledge through formal education, work experience or personal research.
3. Approach to learning: A man of vast culture often has a holistic, experiential approach. He seeks new experiences and perspectives to broaden his understanding of the world. A knowledgeable man can have an analytical, systematic approach. He focuses on specific skills and knowledge in one field.
4. Application of knowledge: A man of vast culture can apply his knowledge and insights to his personal relationships, career and hobbies. A knowledgeable man can use his knowledge to solve problems, make informed decisions or advance in his chosen field.

A man of vast culture has a broader, more diverse knowledge base from different sources and experiences. A knowledgeable man can have a more focused or specialized area of expertise, from formal education or work experience.
