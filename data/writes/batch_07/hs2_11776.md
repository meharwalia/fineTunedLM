Mbps means megabits per second. MB/s means megabytes per second. Both are units of internet speed.

**Main units**

- **Mbps (megabits per second):** This unit measures the speed of data transmission over an internet connection. It shows the number of bits that move in one second. It is commonly used for the speed of cable and fiber-optic internet connections. 1 Mbps equals 1,000,000 bits per second.
- **MB/s (megabytes per second):** This unit measures the actual amount of data that moves over an internet connection. It shows the number of bytes that move in one second. It is commonly used for the speed of downloads and uploads. 1 MB/s equals 8 Mbps, because 1 byte has 8 bits.

**Other units**

- **Kbps (kilobits per second):** This unit is like Mbps, but smaller. 1 Kbps equals 1,000 bits per second.
- **Kb/s (kilobytes per second):** This unit is like MB/s, but smaller. 1 Kb/s equals 8 Kbps, because 1 byte has 8 bits.

Different units have different purposes. The speed of an internet connection affects the speed of downloads, uploads and other online activities. If you understand the units, you can make informed decisions about your internet connection. You can then choose the right plan for your needs.
