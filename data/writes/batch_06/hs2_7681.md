Be transparent and honest with your employees, especially about issues that affect their compensation. Tell your prospective customer success manager these points to give him confidence that the stock options are not worth less than at the previous fundraise:

1. Market volatility: Market volatility is normal. It affects the value of stocks across the board. The market crash may cause concern, but market fluctuations are temporary. The value of your company's stock can change quickly as market conditions change.
2. Company performance: Emphasize that the value of the stock options is directly tied to the performance of the company. If the company continues to grow and succeed, the value of the stock options will likely increase over time, even with market volatility.
3. Long-term perspective: Remind him that stock options are a long-term investment. Take a long-term perspective on their value. The market may fluctuate in the short term. The value of the stock options will likely increase as the company grows and succeeds.
4. Equity ownership: Explain that the value of the stock options is not only the current value of the stock. It is also the employee's ownership stake in the company. When the employee exercises the stock options and becomes an owner, he shares in the company's success. He benefits from any future increase in the value of the stock.
5. Confidence in the company: Express your confidence in the company's future. Say that you believe the stock options will be a valuable part of the compensation package and a worthwhile investment. Emphasize that the company is well-positioned in the market.

Clear and confident communication builds trust. It gives employees the confidence to invest in the future of your company.
