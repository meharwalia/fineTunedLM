The OODA loop is powerful because it gives a structured approach to decision-making. You can apply it in a wide variety of situations, from military operations to business strategy to personal life.

John Boyd, a military strategist and fighter pilot, developed the OODA loop. OODA stands for "Observe, Orient, Decide, and Act." The framework emphasizes rapid observation, orientation, decision-making, and action to gain and keep the advantage in any competitive situation.

The loop helps individuals and organizations adapt constantly to changing circumstances and stay ahead of competitors or opponents. They observe their environment, orient themselves to the situation, decide quickly, and act. This gives them a competitive advantage and helps them achieve their goals.

People have adopted the OODA loop in a wide range of fields. It remains a popular framework for strategic thinking and decision-making.
