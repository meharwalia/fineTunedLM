Here are six ways to cook pork belly:

| Cooking Method | Description | Cooking Time | Temperature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Braising | Cook in broth, wine, or juice in a covered dish. The result is tender, juicy pork with strong flavor. | 2-4 hours | 325°F |
| Roasting | Cook uncovered in the oven with a small amount of liquid. The result is crispy, golden-brown skin and tender, juicy meat. | 1-2 hours | 400°F |
| Grilling | Cook over direct heat on a grill. The result is crispy, golden-brown skin and tender, juicy meat. | 20-30 minutes | Medium-high heat |
| Smoking | Cook over indirect heat. Smoke from wood or other materials adds flavor. The result is tender, juicy meat with a smoky flavor. | 4-6 hours | 225°F |
| Stir-frying | Cook in a wok or skillet over high heat for a short time. The result is crispy, golden-brown skin and tender, juicy meat. | 5-7 minutes | High heat |
| Deep-frying | Cook in hot oil until crispy and golden brown. The result is crispy, crunchy skin and tender, juicy meat. | 5-7 minutes | 350°F |
