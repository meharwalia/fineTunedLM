To immerse yourself in computer science (CS), use these methods:

1. Learn the basics. Learn the fundamentals of programming: variables, data types, control structures, and functions. Start with a simple programming language like Python or JavaScript. Both are easy to learn and widely used in different industries.
2. Work on projects. Apply what you learn. A project can be a simple website or a complex algorithm that solves a specific problem. Projects reinforce your knowledge. They also give you a sense of accomplishment and pride in your work.
3. Join online communities and attend events. Attend conferences, hackathons, and meetups. There you can network with other professionals and learn about the latest trends and developments in the industry.
4. Take online courses and tutorials. Free resources such as Coursera, Udemy, and edX offer courses on a variety of CS topics. Experienced professionals often teach these courses. They give you a structured learning experience.
5. Stay up to date with the latest developments in CS. This is essential to remain competitive in the field. Read blogs, articles, and books. Follow industry leaders and influencers on social media.

Together, these methods help you develop a deep understanding of CS and prepare you for a successful career in the industry.
