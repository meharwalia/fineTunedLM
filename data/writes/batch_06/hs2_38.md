Use these methods to prevent scraping of your site data:

1. robots.txt: Use the robots.txt file to tell web crawlers not to access specific pages or directories. This does not stop all scrapers. It can discourage casual ones.
2. CAPTCHA: Use CAPTCHA to make sure that only human users access your data. It can be an effective deterrent. It can also inconvenience legitimate users.
3. Anti-scraping tools: These tools detect and block scraping activity. Configure them to detect and block specific types of traffic, such as automated bots.
4. Rate limits: Limit the number of requests to your website in a set period. This can prevent scrapers from overwhelming your site and taking down your server.
5. JavaScript: Use JavaScript to hide specific elements on your page or to generate content dynamically. This makes it harder for scrapers to extract your data.
6. Watermarks: Add watermarks to your content, for example a timestamp or a unique identifier. This makes it harder for scrapers to steal your data.
7. Legal methods: Send cease and desist letters or file lawsuits. This can be expensive and time-consuming.

No method guarantees 100% protection against scraping. Combining multiple methods makes it harder for scrapers to access your data.
