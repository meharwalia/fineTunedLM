The American School in London (ASL) and Taipei American School (TAS) are both international schools with an American curriculum. These are the key differences:

1. Location: ASL is in London, England. TAS is in Taipei, Taiwan.
2. Size: ASL has about 1,000 students. TAS has about 1,500 students.
3. Curriculum: Both follow an American curriculum. ASL offers the International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme. TAS does not.
4. Language: Both use English as the language of instruction. TAS also offers Mandarin Chinese as a second language.
5. Extracurricular activities: Both offer a wide range of activities, including sports, music, and art. TAS offers more clubs and activities.
6. Cost: ASL tuition is higher than TAS tuition. Both schools have a similar range of fees for textbooks, meals, and extracurricular activities.
7. Admissions: Both have a competitive process. TAS is more selective, with a higher average SAT score and a higher percentage of students accepted.

Both schools are highly regarded and offer a high-quality American education. The choice depends on location, curriculum, language of instruction, extracurricular activities, cost, and admissions requirements.
