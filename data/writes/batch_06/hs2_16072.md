Try these:

- Turn the screws with needle-nosed pliers. Use little force, which can damage the screws or the casing. For more grip, put a rubber band around the screw.
- Make a DIY screwdriver with a melted plastic handle, or buy a specialized screw removal tool.
- Use a flathead screwdriver if the screw head allows it. Do not strip the screw head.
- Try common household items that fit the screw head, such as a coin or a butter knife.
