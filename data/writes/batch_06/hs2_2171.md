Reply in a way that fits the context and the tone of the stranger's message. These are possible responses:

1. "You're welcome!" A simple and straightforward response. It suits most situations.
2. "No problem!" A more casual response. Use it when the context is relaxed and informal.
3. "Happy to help!" This shows you are pleased to have helped the stranger. It also shows you are friendly and helpful by nature.
4. "My pleasure!" Slightly more formal than the others. Use it when the context is more professional or when you want to show you take pride in being helpful.
5. "Anytime!" Use it when the context is casual and you want to show you can help the stranger again in the future.

The best response depends on the specific context, the tone of the stranger's message, and your own style and preferences. Most important, be polite and respectful.
