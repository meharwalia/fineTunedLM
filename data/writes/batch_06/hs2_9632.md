Data is the actual information that a system stores and processes. Metadata is information about the data itself. They are two different concepts in information management.

Data is raw information. It can be text, numbers, images, videos, or audio files. A system usually stores it in a database or other storage mechanism.

People use it for purposes such as analysis, reporting, or processing.

Metadata gives context about the data, such as its source, format, and structure. It can also include information about the data's quality, accuracy, and currency. It helps users understand and use the data effectively.

**Example of data**

- Customer name: John Smith
- Customer address: 123 Main Street
- Customer phone number: 555-123-4567

**Example of metadata**

- Source of data: Customer database
- Date of data collection: March 1, 2023
- Data format: CSV
- Data quality score: 90%

The data is the information about the customer (name, address, phone number). The metadata shows where the data came from, when it was collected, how it is formatted, and its quality.
