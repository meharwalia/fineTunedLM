The first section depends on your circumstances, the job, and the impression you want to make. These are the common options:

1. Personal information: Your name, address, phone number, and email address. Make sure it is accurate and current.
2. Summary statement: A brief overview of your skills and experience. It can attract the employer's attention and show your most relevant qualifications.
3. Work experience: This is usually the most important section, so it often goes first. If you have limited work experience or change careers, consider putting another section first.
4. Education: Consider putting it first if you have a strong educational background. This applies most to jobs in academia or other fields that value education highly.
5. Skills: This section shows the skills the employer wants. Make sure the skills are relevant to the job.

Try different orders. Keep the one that works best for you.
