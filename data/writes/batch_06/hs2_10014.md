Innovation is the process of creating and implementing something new that generates value. This definition has three key components:

1. Newness: Innovation creates something new or novel. It can be a new product, service, process, or way of doing things. It can also apply existing knowledge or technology in a new way to solve a problem or meet a need.
2. Implementation: Innovation is not only about coming up with new ideas. It is also about putting those ideas into action. Develop a plan to make the new idea a reality. Then execute the plan. This requires a commitment to see the innovation through from start to finish.
3. Value creation: Innovation creates value. The value can take different forms, such as increased revenue, improved customer satisfaction, reduced costs, or enhanced competitive advantage. The innovation must create value for someone: the organization, its customers, its employees, or society as a whole.

Innovation combines creativity, planning, and execution. It focuses on delivering benefits to stakeholders.
