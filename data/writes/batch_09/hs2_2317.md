Data reports and dashboards both visualize and communicate data. They differ in five ways:

1. **Purpose:** Data reports give a detailed analysis of data. Dashboards give a quick and easy overview of key metrics and trends.
2. **Level of detail:** Data reports contain more detailed information and analysis. Dashboards are more concise. They focus on key metrics and trends.
3. **Format:** Data reports are usually static, such as a PDF or Word document. Dashboards are interactive. Users can drill down into the data for more details.
4. **Timeliness:** Dashboards give real-time data and insights. Systems usually generate data reports on a schedule, so they can be less timely.
5. **Customization:** You can customize dashboards for specific user needs. Data reports are usually more standardized and harder to customize.
