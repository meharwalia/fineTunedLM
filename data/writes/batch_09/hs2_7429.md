The difference is in the placement of the adverb "rather" and the preposition "than".

- **First sentence:** "rather than" compares the current behavior of Beijing to that of a red-hot bull. The sentence implies that Beijing must change its behavior and start to act more like a Zen Buddhist master. This is the more desirable quality.
- **Second sentence:** "than" compares the desired behavior of Beijing to that of a red-hot bull. The sentence implies that Beijing must behave like a Zen Buddhist master. This is a more desirable quality than being a red-hot bull.

More examples:

- I prefer to read books rather than watch TV. (books are more desirable than TV)
- She behaves more like a teacher than a student. (teacher is more desirable than student)
- He speaks more like a politician than a scientist. (politician is more desirable than scientist)
- I feel more like exercising rather than sitting around. (exercising is more desirable than sitting around)
- She seems more like a leader rather than a follower. (leader is more desirable than follower)
