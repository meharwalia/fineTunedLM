Here is a simple use case for home renovation.

A homeowner wants to update their kitchen and bathroom. They collect ideas and inspiration from home improvement websites, magazines, and social media. They list the specific changes they want: new cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, and lighting.

Next, the homeowner contacts contractors to get estimates for the work. They compare the estimates. They choose a contractor with a good balance of quality and affordability.

The contractor and team remove the old kitchen and bathroom fixtures and materials. They then install the new cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, and lighting. They make any necessary modifications to the space for the new features.

During the project, the homeowner works closely with the contractor to decide on materials, colors, and finishes. The homeowner tells the contractor about any concerns or issues. This keeps the project on track and meets their expectations.

When the renovation is complete, the homeowner has a new kitchen and bathroom that better suit their needs and preferences. They enjoy the updated spaces and the added value to their home.
