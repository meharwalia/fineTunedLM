Tuberculosis (TB) is a bacterial infection. It primarily affects the lungs. It can also affect other parts of the body.

It spreads through the air when an infected person coughs or sneezes. TB is a serious public health concern in many parts of the world.

**Symptoms**

- Coughing up blood
- Chest pain
- Fatigue
- Weight loss
- Fever

TB can be fatal if you do not treat it.

**Prevention**

- Get vaccinated.
- Practice good hygiene.
- Avoid contact with infected people.

**Treatment**

Treatment typically involves a combination of antibiotics and other medications. Take the medication as prescribed. Complete the full course of treatment, even if symptoms improve. This prevents drug-resistant strains of TB.

Also protect others from the infection:

- Wear a mask in public.
- Isolate yourself from others.
- Wash your hands often.

If you suspect you have TB, seek medical attention.
