Use these six methods to save on Databricks costs without harming team productivity:

1. **Optimize cluster usage.** Shut down clusters when nobody uses them. Use autoscaling to adjust the number of workers to the workload. Use smaller clusters for development and testing. Use larger clusters for production workloads.
2. **Use spot instances.** Spot instances can cost much less than on-demand instances. AWS can end spot instances at any time if demand for them increases. Use them only for workloads that can tolerate interruptions.
3. **Use Databricks cost-saving features.** Job scheduling runs jobs at off-peak hours. Workspace idle timeout shuts down the workspace after a period of inactivity.
4. **Monitor and optimize usage.** Monitor your Databricks usage regularly. Look for jobs that use more resources than they need. Look for teams that leave clusters running when they do not use them. Fixing these problems can reduce your costs significantly.
5. **Use Databricks pricing tiers.** Databricks offers different pricing tiers. One is a free tier with limited use of Databricks clusters. Evaluate your usage carefully. Choose the right tier to save money and still give your teams the resources they need.
6. **Train teams and share best practices.** Make sure teams know the best practices for Databricks. Make sure they follow optimization guidelines. This reduces unnecessary usage and cost.

These practices reduce your Databricks costs and keep your teams productive and efficient.
