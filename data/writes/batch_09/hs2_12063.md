To block a specific URL such as "http://www.facebook.com" with DefensePro "Signature Protection Filters", follow these steps:

1. Log in to the DefensePro web interface with your administrator credentials.
2. Go to the "Policy" section. Select "Signature Protection Filters".
3. Click the "Add" button to create a new signature.
4. In the "Signature" field, enter a name for the signature (e.g. "Block Facebook").
5. In the "Match" tab, select "URL" as the match type.
6. In the "Value" field, enter the URL you want to block (e.g. "http://www.facebook.com").
7. Optional: specify more options. These include the protocol (http or https), the direction of traffic to block (incoming, outgoing, or both), and the source or destination IP addresses for the filter.
8. Click the "Save" button to save the changes.
9. The signature is now in the list of active signatures. It blocks traffic that matches the URL.

The exact steps can vary with the version of DefensePro and the configuration of your network. Blocking entire websites can affect business operations and productivity. Make sure the policies of your organization and its acceptable use guidelines allow it.
