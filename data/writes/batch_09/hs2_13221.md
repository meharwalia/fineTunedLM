A teacher can use these five methods to find each student's level of understanding:

- **Classroom discussions.** Lead discussions on the topics. Ask students to share their thoughts and ideas. This helps you find misconceptions or misunderstandings.
- **Quizzes and assessments.** Give quizzes and assessments to measure understanding of the material. Use multiple-choice, short answer, or essay questions.
- **Group work.** Assign group projects or activities where students work together to solve problems or complete tasks. This shows how well students apply the concepts.
- **Presentations.** Ask students to explain a concept or topic to the class. This helps you find gaps in their understanding.
- **Reflective writing.** Ask students to reflect on what they learned. Ask them how they can apply it in real-life situations. This shows their level of comprehension and their ability to apply the concepts.

ChatGPT is a valuable resource for students. Teachers still need to engage with students and assess their understanding. A combination of these methods helps teachers find areas where students need extra support or guidance. The teacher can then adjust the teaching.
