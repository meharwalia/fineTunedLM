Here is a suggested plan for week 1 of learning mathematics from scratch to advanced level.

**Monday**

- Review basic arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division). Practice with simple problems.
- Study fractions, decimals, and percentages.
- Learn the order of operations (PEMDAS) for mathematical expressions.

**Tuesday**

- Study algebraic expressions: variables, coefficients, and exponents.
- Practice solving simple equations and inequalities.
- Learn how to graph linear equations on a coordinate plane.

**Wednesday**

- Study geometry: points, lines, angles, and shapes.
- Learn how to calculate the areas and volumes of simple shapes.
- Practice measuring lengths, angles, and distances in metric units.

**Thursday**

- Study probability and statistics.
- Learn how to calculate the probabilities of simple events.
- Practice collecting and analyzing data with histograms and box plots.

**Friday**

- Review the topics of this week. Practice solving problems with all the concepts.
- Start to study the topics for next week: linear equations, functions, and graphs.

This is a basic plan. Adjust it to your learning style and pace. Practice and review the concepts every week. With time, you can master mathematics at an advanced level.
