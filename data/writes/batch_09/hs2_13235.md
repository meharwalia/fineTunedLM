**Initial situation**

People depend more and more on electronic devices. The need for accessible power sources is growing. Traditional electrical outlets are limited in number and location. It is hard to charge multiple devices at once or in a convenient location.

**Ideal situation**

A piece of furniture combines the functions of a chair and an electrical outlet. It gives comfortable seating and easy access to power. Users charge their devices while they work, study, or relax. They do not search for a nearby outlet or deal with tangled cords.

**Proposed solution**

Create a prototype of a chair with built-in electrical outlets. Use fablab tools and machinery. The chair has a sleek and modern design. The outlets are in the armrests or backrest. The outlets have USB ports. Users charge their devices directly without an adapter.

**Expected functions**

1. Comfortable seating option
2. Built-in electrical outlets
3. USB ports for direct charging
4. Sleek and modern design
5. Easy to use and maintain

**Elements to test with users**

1. Comfort of the chair
2. Accessibility of the outlets
3. Ease of use of the USB ports
4. Aesthetics of the design
5. Durability and maintenance requirements

**Iterations**

1. **Initial design:** Create a basic prototype of the chair with built-in outlets and USB ports. Test the comfort of the chair. Test the accessibility of the outlets.
2. **Design revision:** Use the user feedback to improve the comfort and the accessibility of the outlets. Test the ease of use of the USB ports. Test the aesthetics of the design.
3. **Final design:** Use the user feedback to create a final prototype. Test the durability and maintenance requirements of the chair.

This iterative design process makes sure the final prototype meets the needs and expectations of the users. It gives a functional and stylish seating option with easy access to power.
