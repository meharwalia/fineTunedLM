You have worked on your game and learned new skills every day for two years. Feeling lost about your direction is a common challenge for game developers. These five suggestions can help you move forward:

1. **Define the core concept of your game.** Ask yourself what your game is about. Ask what core idea you want to explore. A clear core concept makes it easier to decide the direction and the features to focus on.
2. **Break your game into smaller tasks.** Make the tasks manageable. This helps you prioritize your effort. It also helps you avoid feeling overwhelmed.
3. **Focus on one task at a time.** Work on a task until it is complete. This helps you make steady progress. It also stops new ideas or distractions from pulling you away.
4. **Get feedback from others.** Feedback helps you find areas to improve. It can also give you new ideas for features or direction. Join a game development community, or ask friends or family for feedback.
5. **Experiment and iterate.** Game development is an iterative process. Try new ideas. Get feedback. Adjust your game. This helps you refine your game and find the right direction over time.

Game development is a journey. It is normal to feel lost or unsure at times. If you stay focused, seek feedback, and experiment with new ideas, you can overcome these challenges. You can then create a game that you are proud of.
