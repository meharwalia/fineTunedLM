Bio credits are points in a special bank for your body.

Imagine a special bank where you deposit and withdraw points called "bio credits". These points are like money for your body's energy and health.

You earn bio credits when you do good deeds for your body. Eat healthy food. Exercise. Get enough sleep.

Your body uses these credits to stay strong and healthy.

You lose bio credits when you do bad deeds for your body. Eating junk food, sitting too much, or not getting enough sleep costs you credits. You can then feel tired and sick.

You save money in a bank. In the same way, you want to save bio credits in your body's bank to stay healthy and strong!
