The ecological contradiction at the heart of capitalist society is this: the economy depends on nature, but it also divides the two ontologically.

Capitalism needs nature to produce the resources and services it needs to function. It also denies nature any value in its own right. Capitalism seeks to extract as much value as possible from nature. It ignores the long-term consequences. The system is programmed to deny the ecological reproduction costs it generates. This destabilizes ecosystems. It eventually disrupts the entire capitalist society.

Capitalism depends on nature and denies its value. This makes it a cannibal that devours its own vital organs. The result is a cycle of destruction and crisis.
