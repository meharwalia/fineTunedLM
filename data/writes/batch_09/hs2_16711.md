**Lines in Egyptian art**

Ancient Egyptians used lines in art to create a sense of order and stability. This reflects the religious and philosophical beliefs of their culture.

Artists used horizontal and vertical lines to show flat surfaces and maintain balance. They used diagonal lines sparingly to suggest movement or depth.

**Overview of ancient Egyptian history**

Ancient Egyptian history is vast and complex. It spans over 3,000 years, from around 3100 BCE to 332 BCE. The civilization is known for impressive architectural achievements, such as the pyramids and temples. It is also known for rich cultural and religious traditions.

The Egyptians developed writing, mathematics, and medicine. Their art and artifacts still fascinate scholars and visitors today.
