To manage and start a learning program for your club members, follow these steps:

1. **Define the skills to focus on.** Identify the key skills you want to help your members develop. They can be programming languages or soft skills.
2. **Create a learning plan.** List the specific skills you want to teach. List the steps members must take to develop them. Include online courses, readings, or other learning resources.
3. **Assign mentors.** Choose experienced club members or outside experts as mentors. Mentors give guidance, feedback, and support to members.
4. **Track progress.** Track the progress of your members. Ask them to track their own progress. This shows you which members progress and which need extra support.
5. **Celebrate success.** Recognize the achievements of your members. This motivates them to continue to learn.

**Program name**

Call the program "Velocity Academy" or "Velocity Learning Program". Choose a name that shows the focus on learning and development. The name must also link to your club name.

Good luck with your learning program.
