Agile software development is a methodology based on iterative and incremental development. Requirements and solutions evolve through collaboration between self-organizing, cross-functional teams. Agile emphasizes seven principles:

1. **Customer satisfaction.** The team delivers working software frequently. Customers give feedback and change the requirements as the project progresses.
2. **Collaboration.** The development team, stakeholders, and customers work together. Everyone works toward a common goal.
3. **Self-organizing teams.** The development team has the freedom to organize itself. This makes the team more productive and creative.
4. **Frequent feedback.** Frequent testing and feedback make sure the software has high quality. They also make sure it meets the needs of the customers.
5. **Iterative and incremental development.** The team breaks the project into small, incremental steps called iterations. This allows frequent testing and feedback. It also lets the team adapt to changes in requirements.
6. **Adaptive planning.** The team creates a plan at the start of the project. The plan stays flexible and changes as the project progresses.
7. **Continuous improvement.** The team constantly looks for ways to improve its processes and the software.

Agile has become more popular in recent years. It allows greater flexibility and adaptability in software development projects. It has proven effective in delivering high-quality software on time and within budget.
