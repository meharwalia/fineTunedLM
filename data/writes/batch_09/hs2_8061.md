Here are five focus group questions for pediatricians. They can help you understand why psychiatric consultation programs see more white, cisgender, and high socioeconomic status (SES) patients.

1. What do you think are the main reasons that providers refer pediatric patients from specific demographic groups for psychiatric consultation more than others?
2. How do you think societal and cultural factors influence the mental health needs of different pediatric patient populations?
3. What role do healthcare access and insurance coverage play in which pediatric patients receive psychiatric consultation services?
4. How do you discuss mental health concerns with parents and caregivers from different demographic groups?
5. What challenges do you face in providing mental health care to pediatric patients from diverse backgrounds? How do you try to overcome them?

These questions can give you insights into the factors that contribute to this overrepresentation. They can also help you find areas to improve equity and access to mental health care for all pediatric patients.
